How to play

Guess the word in six tries.

  • Each guess must be a real word of the chosen length — and never a name.
  • The colour of the tiles shows how close your guess was.
CROWN

C is in the word and in the right spot.

STEEL

RAVEN

V is not in the word at all.

A new daily word for each length arrives at midnight — or play unlimited any time.
