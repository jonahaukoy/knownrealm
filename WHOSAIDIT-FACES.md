# Who Said It — the faces to draw

Every face here hangs on the wall of the Hall of Faces when you name that character correctly, or
appears as one of the four portraits you choose between. They are drawn from the site-wide portrait
table (`js/people.js`), so a face you draw once is used by the maps, the wiki and every game at once
— you are never drawing anything twice.

**Pool as it stands: 257 quotes, 89 distinct speakers, 137 names in total.**

There are two different jobs here, and only the first one is about the wall.

---

## Job 1 — the 89 speakers (these hang on the wall)

A face is hung only when that character is the *answer*. This is the list that fills the hall.

It is front-loaded, so you do not have to draw all 89 to get most of the benefit:

| If you draw | the wall is filled for |
|---|---|
| the top 10 | 34% of all rounds |
| the top 20 | 51% |
| the top 25 | 58% |
| the top 40 | 75% |
| all 89 | 100% |

## If 89 is too many

Say the word and I will **cut the Tier 3 speakers down to the ones you want to draw**, moving their
quotes to a speaker you have already drawn where the line allows it, and dropping the rest. That
would take the wall's face count from 89 to 56 and cost about 33 quotes out of 257.

The same is true of the wrong-answer list: I can rewrite the distractors on any question to reuse
faces you have already drawn, which would shrink Job 2 substantially without touching a single
quote. That is probably the cheapest win available — tell me the number of faces you are willing to
draw and I will fit the pool to it.
