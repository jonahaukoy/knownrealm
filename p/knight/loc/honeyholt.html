Honeyholt

Seat of House Beesbury

The Beesburys' seat amid orchards and hives on the Honeywine — home of young Ser Humfrey, who stands with Dunk at Ashford.

The fuller tale

Honeyholt sits amid orchards and hives on the Honeywine, seat of House Beesbury, a family whose entire heraldic identity — and, by most accounts, a fair portion of their actual income — runs on honey rather than on anything more martial. Young Ser Humfrey of Honeyholt stands among the seven who fight and bleed for Dunk's cause at Ashford Meadow, a small act of decency from a minor house that costs the Beesburys rather more than a jar of their famous honey ever could.
