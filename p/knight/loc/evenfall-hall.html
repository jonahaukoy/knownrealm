Evenfall Hall

Seat of House Tarth

Seat of the Tarths on the Sapphire Isle, said to be the loveliest island in the narrow sea.

The fuller tale

Evenfall Hall crowns the Sapphire Isle of Tarth, reputed by sailors and songs alike to be the loveliest island anywhere in the narrow sea — blue waters, green hills, and a castle that has the luxury, unlike most stormlands seats, of looking out on scenery rather than a border. House Tarth holds it quietly in this era, a minor lordship whose chief business with the wider realm is supplying the narrow sea’s shipping lanes with safe anchorage rather than supplying the tourney field with champions; Evenfall Hall’s claim to fame in Dunk and Egg’s age is simply that it is, by universal agreement, a very pleasant place to be forgotten by history for a while.
