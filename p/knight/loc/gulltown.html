Gulltown

Port city of the Vale

The Vale's harbor city, held by House Grafton for the Arryns.

The fuller tale

Gulltown is the Vale’s working harbor, a trading city held for the Arryns by House Grafton, whose fortunes rise and fall with shipping tolls and salt-fish prices rather than with the mountain politics that occupy the Eyrie above. It is, in effect, the Vale’s door to the wider realm — the Eyrie handles the Vale’s pride, Gulltown handles its commerce — and in an era when the crown’s peace has made travel and trade unusually safe, that door does brisker business than it has in a generation.

The fallen of this place — if any, they are named here. Spoilers.

No death the chronicle records has come to this ground — or none the maesters have set down.
