Raventree Hall

Seat of House Blackwood

Seat of the old-gods-keeping Blackwoods — kin to Bloodraven, whose mother was a Blackwood, and forever feuding with the Brackens across the river.

The fuller tale

Raventree Hall belongs to House Blackwood, keepers of the old gods in a riverlands otherwise gone over to the Faith of the Seven generations ago, their godswood’s black-barked weirwoods as much a statement of stubborn identity as of religion. The house carries a closer tie to the era’s great events than its remote godswood might suggest — Brynden Rivers, Bloodraven himself, is Blackwood on his mother’s side, a bloodline the Blackwoods mention with considerably more pride now that their kinsman runs the Iron Throne’s intelligence network than they might have when he was simply one of the old king’s many bastards. Their ancient feud with the Brackens across the river burns on regardless, old gods against new grievances, largely undisturbed by whichever Targaryen currently sits the throne.
