The fuller tale

Wyl is a marcher seat deep in the Boneway, old in border blood and home country to the very brigands hanged at Stoney Sept on Dunk and Egg's road to Whitewalls ÔÇö a house whose relationship with the crown's law has, across generations of raiding the stormlands passes, run rather warmer toward its own outlaws than toward the crown's justice. Wyl produces knights and lords like any other house; it has simply never been especially embarrassed about the family's other trade as well.
