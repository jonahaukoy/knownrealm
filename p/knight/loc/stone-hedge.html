Stone Hedge

Seat of House Bracken

The Brackens' seat on the Red Fork — kin to Bittersteel, whose mother was a Bracken, and forever feuding with the Blackwoods across the river.

The fuller tale

Stone Hedge answers Raventree Hall from the opposite bank of the Red Fork, seat of House Bracken and the Blackwoods’ hereditary enemy in a feud so old neither side entirely remembers the original grievance, only the centuries of raided cattle and burned fields piled on top of it. The Brackens claim their own kinship to the era’s great and terrible men — Aegor Rivers, called Bittersteel, the Blackfyre loyalists’ fiercest commander, is Bracken on his mother’s side, a mirror to Bloodraven’s Blackwood blood that turns the ancient riverlands rivalry into an uncomfortable echo of the larger dynastic war being fought, on and off, across the whole realm.
