Golden Tooth

Gate of the Westerlands

The small strong castle that locks the hill road between the Riverlands and the West.

The fuller tale

Golden Tooth is a small, strongly built castle commanding the hill road between the riverlands and the westerlands, held by House Lefford in a position that matters far more than the castle’s modest size would suggest — every army, merchant train, or wedding party moving between the two regions by land passes beneath its walls, and the Leffords have made a comfortable living out of being the gate nobody can simply walk around.

The fallen of this place — if any, they are named here. Spoilers.

No death the chronicle records has come to this ground — or none the maesters have set down.
