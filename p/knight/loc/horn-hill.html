Horn Hill

Seat of House Tarly

The Tarlys' seat beneath the foothills of the Red Mountains.

The fuller tale

Horn Hill lies beneath the foothills of the Red Mountains, seat of House Tarly, a family already known in this era for producing capable, hard-nosed commanders rather than merely decorative lords — a reputation the house has been quietly building for generations before it becomes the stuff of wider legend. In Dunk and Egg's age Horn Hill is simply one more well-run Reach holding, its martial seriousness a matter of local reputation rather than realm-wide fame just yet.

The fallen of this place — if any, they are named here. Spoilers.
