The fuller tale

Cider Hall sits where the Mander meets the Cockleswhent, seat of a Fossoway branch prosperous enough to name a castle for its orchards, and it enters the tale as the origin point of a joke the wider saga never lets die. The quarrel outlives the tourney and splits the house cleanly down the middle, red apple against green, a division the family never quite mends and the rest of Westeros never quite lets them forget.

Cider Hall itself is unremarkable as castles go ÔÇö a comfortable holding among the orchards of the upper Mander, more concerned with cider presses than politics ÔÇö which is precisely what makes its accidental role in the Fossoway split so telling. It took one hedge knight, one insult, and one moment of decency at a tourney gate to hand a minor Reach house a permanent identity crisis; the red apple and the green apple both still fly, generations on, arguing without ever quite remembering that the argument began here.
