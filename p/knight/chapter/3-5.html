The chapter retold

The tourney opens with the lists full of old Blackfyre men, and two knights cut against the grain of it. Ser Glendon Ball — the Knight of the Pussywillows, Fireball's angry, baseborn son — unhorses all comers with a fury that has something to prove; and Dunk, meaning to win the egg to keep it out of worse hands, falls instead to the craft of Ser Uthor Underleaf, the Snail, and wakes with a dented helm and a ransom he cannot pay. The chapter is a lesson in the tilt as a trade rather than a sport, and it strands Dunk in a hostile castle owing money he does not have.
