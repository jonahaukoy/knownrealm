The chapter retold

On the kingsroad they fall in with a wedding party bound for Whitewalls, and the company tells you everything if you know how to read it: sour Lord Gormon Peake, amiable Ser Alyn Cockshaw, and above all the golden Ser John the Fiddler ÔÇö who laughs easily, jousts brilliantly, and unsettles Dunk by claiming to have dreamed of him before. The chapter is all charm and unease, the Fiddler's warmth pulling Dunk in while Peake's cold watchfulness warns him off, and the reader begins to suspect that a hedge knight has wandered into the middle of something far larger than a country wedding.
