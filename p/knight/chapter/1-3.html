The chapter retold

Ashford Meadow opens before them like a city grown overnight ÔÇö a hundred pavilions along the Cockleswhent, merchants and mummers and the fair maid's painted viewing stand. Dunk makes his camp beneath a great elm and meets the Dornish puppeteers, chief among them the tall girl Tanselle, who paints and works a wooden dragon on strings and lets a wooden hero's lance kill it to the crowd's delight. The chapter is the saga at its most inviting, all color and possibility ÔÇö and it quietly introduces the two things that will nearly cost Dunk his life: a puppet dragon, and a girl kind enough to paint a poor knight's shield.
