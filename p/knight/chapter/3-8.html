The Mystery Knight — Dunk VIII

Ser Alyn Cockshaw draws Dunk aside to a quiet well — and pays bravos' wages for it. Ser Maynard Plumm proves quicker: Alyn goes down the well, and Plumm shows Dunk that he knows exactly who Egg is, and minds not at all.

The chapter retold

Ser Alyn Cockshaw draws Dunk aside to a quiet well and pays bravos' wages to see him dead there — and Ser Maynard Plumm proves the quicker hand, sending Cockshaw down the well instead. The chapter's real revelation follows: Plumm shows Dunk that he knows exactly who Egg is, a prince of the blood in a hedge knight's train, and minds not at all — a man playing his own unreadable game amid everyone else's. Dunk survives an assassination only to discover how many people at Whitewalls have already seen through his squire's shaven head.
