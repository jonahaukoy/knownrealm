The chapter retold

The final completed novella opens in a realm holding its breath. Near Stoney Sept, Dunk and Egg ride past gibbets heavy with hanged brigands ÔÇö Bloodraven's justice, displayed where every traveler can read it ÔÇö and the talk on the road is all of traitors hunted and the black dragon stirring again across the narrow sea.
