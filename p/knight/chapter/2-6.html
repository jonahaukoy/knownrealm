The chapter retold

The widow's terms are plain: she will have Bennis's blood for the digger's cheek, and Ser Eustace's pride will have his stream. And then the old knight confesses the thing Dunk has half-guessed ÔÇö at the Redgrass Field, where the first Blackfyre Rebellion was drowned in blood, the chequy lion flew for the black dragon, and Eustace's sons died on the losing side. Dunk's sworn service turns to a stone in his chest: he has bound himself to a traitor's cause without knowing it, and the chapter forces the saga's central question on him ÔÇö what an oath is worth once you learn the truth of the man you swore it to.
