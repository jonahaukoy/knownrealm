The chapter retold

In his cell Dunk learns the price the law sets on his fists: a hand and a foot, the parts that struck the prince. Aerion, not content to see him merely maimed, demands a trial of seven ÔÇö the old Andal way, seven champions a side ÔÇö which means Dunk must find six men willing to fight and perhaps die for a hedge knight nobody has heard of. The chapter is a bleak accounting of what a promise is worth once it is called in: Raymun Fossoway shames his cousin Steffon into pledging aid, Steffon sells the pledge to Aerion by morning for the promise of advancement, and Raymun is knighted at dawn to stand in the traitor's place ÔÇö the first green apple of a branch that will outlast the rotten red one.
