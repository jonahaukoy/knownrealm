The chapter retold

The chequy lion rides to war with an army of five, and at the Chequy Water it meets the spider's column ÔÇö three-and-thirty knights, crossbowmen, and the Longinch at their head. The chapter is a study in how close a stupid quarrel can come to a massacre: one wrong word from either bank and the marches drown in smallfolk blood, plowmen against knights over a stream that means nothing to anyone by winter. Dunk stands in the middle of it, sworn to the weaker side, searching for the single thing that might keep it from becoming a slaughter.

  • Dunk makes the quarrel single combat: himself against the Longinch, in the middle of the disputed water, before the eyes of gods, knights, and diggers.
