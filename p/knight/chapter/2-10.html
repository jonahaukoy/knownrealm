The chapter retold

Dunk wakes at Coldmoat, leeched and alive, to find the war ended the oldest way of all — with a wedding. Ser Eustace Osgrey and Lady Rohanne Webber are wed in a sept, the stream restored to Standfast and both houses' honor mended in the same breath, a settlement that makes a quiet mockery of every drop of blood the quarrel cost. And the Red Widow pays a hedge knight's fee with a kiss, and keeps a lock of his hair pressed in her book of debts, before Dunk and Egg take to the road again — the novella closing on the small, human ambiguity that is the saga's signature.

This movement: The Chequy Water

  • Two armies meet at a stolen stream: five men behind a chequy lion, thirty-three behind a spotted spider — and the marches a spark away from smallfolk slaughter.
  • Dunk makes the quarrel single combat: himself against the Longinch, in the middle of the disputed water, before the eyes of gods, knights, and diggers.
  • Steel in the stream: Dunk is cut, battered, and half-drowned — and Ser Lucas Inchfield is dead in the water at the end of it.
  • Peace the old way: Ser Eustace weds Lady Rohanne, the stream flows to Standfast again — and the Red Widow settles a hedge knight's fee with a kiss and keeps a lock of his hair.
