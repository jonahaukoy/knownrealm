Raymun Fossoway

House Fossoway

A squire of Cider Hall with a better heart than his knighted cousin — the sort of man who is embarrassed by other men's dishonor. The green-apple Fossoways begin with him.

A soul of House Fossoway, born at Cider Hall.

The fuller tale

Season One

In A Trial of Seven — Knighted at dawn by the Laughing Storm — the first green-apple Fossoway. Raymun Fossoway shames his cousin Steffon into promising help; Steffon sells his promise to Aerion instead by morning — and Raymun is knighted on the spot to take his place. In The Judgment of the Gods — Green apple against red: he settles it with his cousin directly. The lances shatter and the melee closes in. The Laughing Storm bellows; Raymun finds his cousin Steffon and pays the family debt in steel.

Where they stand. Cider Hall and Ashford Meadow.

How it ends for them

The chronicle records no death for this soul — they are living when the tale leaves them, or their ending is a page the maesters have yet to turn.

Dunk VIII In his cell Dunk learns the price of striking a dragon: a hand and a foot. Aerion demands a trial of seven. Dunk begs the meadow for champions; Steffon Fossoway sells his promise to the prince, and his cousin Raymun is knighted at dawn to stand in his place.

Where the page and the screen part

Shaun Thomas plays Raymun Fossoway in the show’s first season, part of the tournament’s supporting cast of young knights and squires whose smaller storylines flesh out Ashford Meadow beyond its royal principals — a faithful, if necessarily compressed, rendering of a character whose page presence is itself fairly brief.

Their journey

Where the two tellings send them to the same place the road runs whole; where they part, the screen keeps the left and the books the right.
