How it ends for them

On the page. Cut down by Dunk at the gates of Whitewalls (TMK, ch. 11)

Lord Butterwell's grim good-son held the gates of Whitewalls with treason on his sword when Bloodraven's army ringed the castle. He crossed steel with Ser Duncan the Tall and died of it, in the last fight of the Second Blackfyre Rebellion ÔÇö a rebellion that never managed a battle.

The end comes for him at the gates of Whitewalls, sword in hand, holding a line that has already lost. BloodravenÔÇÖs army has surrounded the castle by the time the fighting starts, and there is no realistic outcome left for the men defending it ÔÇö only the manner of the losing. Ser Duncan the Tall crosses steel with him in the chaos of that last stand, and Black Tom Heddle dies of it, one of the very few casualties in a rebellion that otherwise collapses almost entirely without a pitched battle.
