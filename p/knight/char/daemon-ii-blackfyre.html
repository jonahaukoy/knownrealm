Where the page and the screen part

This character’s identity as the true figure behind John the Fiddler is the central mystery The Mystery Knight is built around, and no season of the show has yet reached that novella, so there is no adaptation to compare against. Whenever the production does reach Whitewalls, the challenge will be considerable: sustaining a reveal that print readers already often see coming, in a visual medium that tends to tip its hand through casting alone.
