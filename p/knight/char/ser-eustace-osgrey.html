Ser Eustace Osgrey

House OsgreyThe Black Dragon's Men

The chequy lion of Standfast: an old knight rich in pride, poor in everything else, who can recite a thousand years of Osgrey glories and cannot stop living in one particular battle. His sons are graves; his lands are three fields and a dry wood.

A soul of House Osgrey, born at Standfast, sworn to The Black Dragon's Men.

Where the page and the screen part

Peter Mullan was cast as Ser Eustace Osgrey in March 2026 for the show’s second season, which will adapt The Sworn Sword — casting widely praised in early coverage for matching the character’s blend of faded grandeur and genuine pathos. As of this writing that season has not yet aired, so there is no performance yet to weigh against the page, but Mullan’s reputation for finding dignity in wounded, stubborn men suggests the production understands exactly what kind of actor this particular role requires.
