Ser Lyonel Baratheon

House Baratheon

The Laughing Storm: heir to Storm's End and one of the great fighters of the age, who bellows with mirth as he unhorses men. Fury and laughter, in House Baratheon, turn out to be the same thing.

A soul of House Baratheon, born at Storm's End.

Season One

In The Lists — The Laughing Storm, unhorsing men with a roar of mirth. In A Trial of Seven — "Ours is the fury" — laughing, he takes his place in Dunk's seven. In The Judgment of the Gods — Fights through the seven with a storm's own laughter.

Where they stand. Storm's End and Ashford Meadow.

How it ends for them

Their chapters, book by book

Where the page and the screen part

Daniel Ings plays Ser Lyonel Baratheon in the show’s first season, and reviewers responded warmly to a performance that leans into the character’s central joke — a warrior whose good humor never once reads as softness — without losing the genuine physical menace the role requires. The Laughing Storm’s tourney bouts were among the season’s more purely enjoyable set pieces, a bit of levity amid a story that turns steadily darker as the trial of seven approaches.

Their journey

Where the two tellings send them to the same place the road runs whole; where they part, the screen keeps the left and the books the right.
