The fuller tale

Their story

A good-natured, thoroughly threadbare hedge knight of Misty Moor, Ser Kyle the Cat travels the road to Lord Butterwell’s wedding in company with Dunk and Egg, carrying with him the one gift he believes might finally win a lord’s favor: an actual cat, offered in the hope that Lord Butterwell’s household will find room for one more hopeful, unremarkable knight in exchange for it.

The gesture is modest almost to the point of comedy, and entirely sincere — Kyle’s whole career amounts to a long series of small hopes like this one, chasing a patron, a position, anything more permanent than the road itself. Hedge knights hope, the tale observes of him with a certain fondness, because hope is very nearly all they have left to spend.

How it ends for them

The chronicle records no death for this soul — they are living when the tale leaves them, or their ending is a page the maesters have yet to turn.

Their chapters, book by book

Where the page and the screen part

Ser Kyle the Cat has not yet appeared in any aired season of the show, his role belonging to The Mystery Knight rather than the already-adapted Hedge Knight, and no casting has been announced. His modest, hopeful presence would offer the production a gentle counterpoint to Whitewalls’ grander conspiracies whenever a future season gets there.
