S3·E2 — Dark Wings, Dark Words

Joffrey I Baratheon holds the throne Hand: Lord Tywin Lannister

The hour retold

Dark wings, dark words: the news is bad everywhere, and the roads are worse. This is the season’s traveling episode, and its gift is pairing people who should never share a fire. Arya’s little band is scooped up by the Brotherhood Without Banners — outlaws with manners — and her cover survives until a captive Hound looks up and names her in front of everyone, the past arriving like a bill. Bran, fleeing north, meets a boy in the woods who has dreamed his dreams: Jojen Reed gives Bran the word for what he is — warg — and with it the first hint that the crippled Stark is not the story’s leftover but its strangest weapon.

The episode’s sharpest edge is on a bridge in the riverlands, where Brienne and her prisoner finally cross swords and Jaime, half-starved and chained, still nearly takes her — until she beats him down with the patience of a woman who has been underestimated her whole life. Locke’s men arrive before the lesson settles, and both of them belong to the Boltons now. In the capital, Sansa is invited to tea by the Tyrells and, coaxed by kindness — a substance she has not encountered in two years — tells the truth out loud for once: Joffrey is a monster. It is the most dangerous sentence she knows, spoken to the only people who asked, and Olenna Tyrell receives it like a woman confirming a market report.

What happens

  • Bran meets Jojen and Meera Reed, and learns the word for what he is: warg.
  • Arya's band is picked up by the Brotherhood Without Banners — and the Hound names her.
  • Brienne and Jaime cross swords on a bridge and lose to Locke's men.
  • Sansa tells the Tyrells the truth about Joffrey: he is a monster.
