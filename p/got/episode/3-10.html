S3·E10 — Mhysa

Joffrey I Baratheon holds the throne Hand: Lord Tywin Lannister

The hour retold

The finale opens in the ashes of the Twins and refuses to look away: the Stark host burned in its camp, Frey men jeering, and the King in the North paraded with his direwolf’s head sewn where his own should be. Arya, watching from the Hound’s saddle, hears a soldier boast of the sewing and takes her first grown life over it — a knife from nowhere, valar morghulis, and the Hound tidying up after her with something close to respect. The kill is small and the moment enormous: the list is no longer a prayer. Elsewhere the ledger of the wedding is settled in daylight — Roose Bolton is Warden of the North, Walder Frey has his floors scrubbed, and Tywin, over dinner, explains to a horrified small council that a wedding was cheaper than a war, which has the disadvantage of being true.

The episode spends its back half naming things. The smiling boy who has spent a season unmaking Theon Greyjoy introduces himself properly — Ramsay Snow, the Bolton bastard — and christens his creature Reek; on Pyke, Balon shrugs at his son’s severed piece, and it is Yara who commandeers a ship to go get what remains of her brother. At Castle Black, Sam gives Bran the last of his dragonglass and watches him pass under the Wall going the wrong direction — the only man alive who knows what waits up there, letting a boy walk into it because the boy says he must. And in the season’s final image Daenerys, outside Yunkai, waits to learn what she has conquered and is answered by a city’s worth of freedmen chanting a word that is not queen: mhysa. Mother. The crowd lifts her like a relic. It is a triumph shot exactly like a warning.

What happens

  • Roose Bolton is Warden of the North; Walder Frey washes his hands and his floors.
  • The smiling boy has a name — Ramsay Snow — and Theon has a new one: Reek.
  • Joffrey crows about serving Robb's head at his wedding; Tywin sends him to bed without supper.
  • Sam gives Bran dragonglass and watches him go under the Wall, north.
  • Yunkai's freedmen call Dany Mhysa — Mother — and carry her on their hands.
