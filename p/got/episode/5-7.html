S5·E7 — The Gift

Tommen I Baratheon holds the throne Hand: Vacant

The hour retold

The Wall loses its oldest resident this hour: Maester Aemon dies murmuring his brother’s childhood name — Egg — a hundred and two years of Targaryen history closing out in a small room, unwitnessed by the throne it might have changed everything to claim. Jon rides for Hardhome in his absence, gambling the Watch’s last credibility on a fishing village of strangers who have spent their lives being told the men in black cloaks are the enemy. Back at Winterfell, Sansa’s single chance at rescue depends on Theon lighting a signal candle in a broken tower — and the show makes the betrayal land harder by making it understandable: Reek has been so thoroughly unmade that loyalty to his own survival outweighs loyalty to a girl he once knew as a friend, and he carries her plea straight to Ramsay instead.

In Meereen, Jorah’s last gambit pays off in the strangest currency the show has offered a queen: not gold, not an army, but Tyrion Lannister, delivered into the fighting pits and bowing with the driest joke he has — I am the gift. It is the meeting the season has been engineering for years, two of the world’s cleverest exiles finally in the same room. And in King’s Landing, Littlefinger’s slow-motion demolition of Cersei reaches its punchline: he hands Olenna a weapon disguised as gossip, and Cersei, visiting Margaery’s cell to gloat, walks out of the sept in chains of her own — the season’s recurring joke about the High Sparrow finally landing on the woman who built his army for him.

What happens

  • Jon rides for Hardhome; Maester Aemon dies at the Wall with Egg's name on his lips.
  • Sansa begs Theon to light the signal candle; he carries the plea to Ramsay instead.
  • Jorah's 'gift' to the queen in a Meereen pit is Tyrion Lannister, alive and bowing.
  • Littlefinger hands Olenna a weapon, and Cersei walks into the sept as a guest — and stays as a prisoner.
