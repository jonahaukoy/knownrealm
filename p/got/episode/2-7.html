S2·E7 — A Man Without Honor

Joffrey I Baratheon holds the throne Hand: Tyrion Lannister, acting Hand

The hour retold

The title is Jaime Lannister’s self-assessment, delivered from a stockade after he has murdered his own cousin for a half-chance at escape — and the hour keeps turning the phrase over like a coin. Honor, in this episode, is mostly the story people tell about the things they were going to do anyway. Theon, humiliated by two missing Stark boys, rides out with hounds and comes back with two small charred bodies to hang from Winterfell’s walls — better to be cruel than weak, he tells himself, having chosen a third option worse than either. The reveal that the bodies are the miller’s boys is withheld; the show lets the audience sit with the worst version, because Theon has to live in it either way.

The captives run the table. Ygritte spends the hour as Jon’s prisoner in name and his interrogator in practice, needling him about vows, freedom and everything under his cloak, until the ambush closes and the roles reverse — you know nothing, Jon Snow, now established as both taunt and diagnosis. In Qarth, Pyat Pree demonstrates the difference between wealth and power by murdering the Thirteen in open council and inviting Dany to the House of the Undying, where her dragons wait. And in the capital, Cersei — rattled by her son and her siege — gives Sansa the only honest tutorial she will ever offer: love no one but your children. In an episode full of men explaining honor, the queen’s cynicism lands as the closest thing to truth on offer.

What happens

  • Jaime murders his own cousin in a cell for one chance at escape — and is dragged back.
  • Theon hunts the missing Stark boys and hangs two burned farm boys from Winterfell's walls.
  • Ygritte talks circles around her captor until the ambush is hers, not his.
  • In Qarth, Pyat Pree kills the Thirteen and names Xaro king; the dragons are in the House of the Undying.
  • Sansa flowers; Cersei offers her the only honest lesson she has: love no one but your children.
