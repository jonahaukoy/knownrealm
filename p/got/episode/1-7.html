S1·E7 — You Win or You Die

Robert I Baratheon †, then Joffrey I holds the throne Hand: Eddard Stark, Protector of the Realm — for a day

The hour retold

Cersei names the game in a garden, and the hour spends its whole length proving her right. Ned, holding proof that her children are Jaime’s, does the most Ned thing imaginable: he tells her what he knows and gives her time to run — mercy extended to the one player who will never return it. Then a boar opens the king on his hunt, and the clock starts. Robert dictates a will naming Ned protector of the realm until “my son” comes of age; Ned, writing, quietly amends it to “my rightful heir,” a correction that is both entirely honest and entirely useless, because paper is only power when someone with swords agrees it is.

Everyone offers Ned a way to win. Renly offers a coup by night with a hundred swords; Littlefinger offers the gold cloaks and a brokered peace with the boy on the throne; Ned refuses both, because one is treason and the other is dishonor, and walks into the throne room with Robert’s will in his hand. Cersei tears it in half and Littlefinger’s knife arrives at Ned’s throat with an apology — he did warn him not to trust him. Meanwhile the story’s other futures lock in: Jon swears his vows before a heart tree and learns stewarding may be a promotion in disguise, and in Essos a wineseller’s clumsy poison attempt turns Drogo’s vague westward ambitions into a khal’s blood-vow. You win or you die, she said. The board is now exactly that simple.

What happens

  • Ned tells Cersei he knows: her children are Jaime's, none of them Robert's.
  • A boar opens the king; Robert dictates his will to "my son" — Ned writes "my rightful heir".
  • Littlefinger promises the gold cloaks; Renly offers a coup; Ned chooses honor.
  • In the throne room Cersei tears the will — "you win or you die" — and Littlefinger's knife finds Ned's throat.
  • Beyond the Wall, Jon says his vows before a heart tree; an assassin's wine fails, and Drogo vows to cross the sea.
