S5·E6 — Unbowed, Unbent, Unbroken

Tommen I Baratheon holds the throne Hand: Vacant

The hour retold

Arya’s first true test at the House of Black and White comes disguised as a kindness: a dying girl asks for water, and the assassin’s apprentice gives her both water and mercy in the same cup, only to be told that neither belonged to her to give. The lesson is the season’s cruelest one about identity — that becoming no one means surrendering the very impulses that make Arya Stark worth following — and the show trusts the audience to feel the wrongness of the trade even as the House insists it is generosity. At Winterfell, the marriage the realm has been dreading arrives at last, and the show stages Ramsay’s wedding-night cruelty with Theon forced to watch — not for spectacle, but to complete the season’s bleakest circle: the boy who broke Winterfell made to witness what breaking a person, entirely and on purpose, actually costs.

Elsewhere the hour distributes smaller reckonings. In Dorne, the rescue mission that has spent an episode building toward violence resolves itself almost apologetically — Myrcella and Trystane’s betrothal survives, Areo Hotah’s axe settles what words couldn’t, and the whole subplot exhales rather than detonates. Olenna Tyrell returns to King’s Landing to buy her grandchildren’s freedom and meets, for once, an opponent with nothing she can purchase — the High Sparrow answers her threats with scripture, and the Queen of Thorns leaves the sept for the first time in her life without having won. And far east, Tyrion and Jorah are sold into slavery and marched toward Meereen’s fighting pits, the dwarf talking his way toward relevance the only way he knows how, one step ahead of the sword.

What happens

  • Arya passes her first test in the House of Black and White and is given a face-lined cellar instead of answers.
  • Jaime and the Sand Snakes reach Myrcella at the same moment; Areo Hotah's axe ends the scuffle.
  • Olenna Tyrell returns for her grandchildren; the Sparrow quotes scripture back at her.
  • Sansa's wedding night in Winterfell — Ramsay makes Theon watch. The North's walls hear it.
