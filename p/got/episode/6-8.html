S6·E8 — No One

Tommen I Baratheon holds the throne Hand: Ser Kevan Lannister

The hour retold

The Hound gets his reckoning in installments this hour, working through the men who destroyed his commune two at a time with an axe and very little conversation, while the Brotherhood Without Banners offers him something rarer than revenge: a cause to point the violence toward. In King’s Landing, Cersei answers the Faith Militant’s growing power with the only argument she trusts, sending the Mountain to remove a septon’s head in front of witnesses, and Tommen — horrified, and finally decisive about something — abolishes trial by combat altogether, quietly and permanently taking away his mother’s last legal escape hatch from the charges waiting for her.

At Riverrun, Jaime solves the siege the way he solves most things now, with a threat instead of a battle — releasing Edmure Tully to reclaim his own castle bloodlessly, while the Blackfish dies offstage, sword in hand, exactly as stubborn in death as he was in every scene he was given. And in Braavos, the season’s longest-running mystery finally resolves in a dark cellar: the Waif hunts a supposedly blind, supposedly broken girl through the House of Black and White’s tunnels, only to discover — far too late — that Arya spent her blindness training for precisely this. A girl is Arya Stark of Winterfell, she tells the man behind the many faces, and this time the House lets her leave rather than argue the point.

What happens

  • The Hound reclaims his axe two heads at a time; the Brotherhood offers him soup and a cause.
  • Cersei chooses violence; the Mountain de-heads a Faith Militant; Tommen abolishes trial by combat.
  • Jaime frees Edmure to take Riverrun bloodlessly; the Blackfish dies sword in hand, offstage and unbowed.
  • The Waif hunts Arya through Braavos — into a dark cellar where a blind girl trained. Valar morghulis; a girl goes home.
