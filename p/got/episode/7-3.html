S7·E3 — The Queen's Justice

Cersei I Lannister holds the throne Hand: Qyburn, Hand of the Queen

The hour retold

Ice finally meets fire in a room at Dragonstone, and the meeting is a stalemate dressed up as diplomacy: Jon Snow will not bend the knee to a queen he has just met, no matter how good her claim or how badly the North needs her dragons, and Daenerys will not simply hand a stranger the one thing every claimant in Westeros has fought and died for. He leaves with dragonglass and nothing resolved, which the show treats as its own kind of progress — two people learning to trust each other in the only currency either has to spare. In King’s Landing, Euron parades Ellaria and Tyene Sand through the streets as trophies, and Cersei repays the Sand Snakes’ murder of Myrcella with a slow, exact cruelty of her own devising, poisoning Tyene with the same kiss that killed her daughter while forcing Ellaria to watch, chained, unable to look away.

The season’s military optimism curdles fast: the Unsullied take Casterly Rock only to find it strategically worthless, the Lannister army long since marched elsewhere, and while Grey Worm’s men celebrate an empty victory, Jaime Lannister’s forces are busy taking Highgarden, the Tyrell breadbasket Dany actually needed. Olenna Tyrell, cornered and doomed either way, drinks Jaime’s offer of a painless poison and spends her very last breath handing him one more piece of information than he wanted — the confession that she, not Tyrion, murdered Joffrey — a final thorn planted with the same dry, unhurried relish she has brought to every scene the show has given her.

What happens

  • Ice meets fire: Jon Snow stands before Daenerys and will not kneel — but leaves with dragonglass.
  • Euron parades Ellaria and Tyene through the capital; Cersei repays a poisoned kiss in kind.
  • The Unsullied take Casterly Rock and find it hollow — the Lannister army is at Highgarden.
  • Olenna Tyrell drinks Jaime's painless poison and confesses to Joffrey with her last breath.
