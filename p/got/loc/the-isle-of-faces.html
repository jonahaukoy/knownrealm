The Isle of Faces

Where the Pact was sworn

The holy island in the Gods Eye where the Pact between the First Men and the children of the forest was sealed beneath the carved weirwoods. No one sails there now; the smallfolk say the green men still keep it, antlered and patient, waiting out the centuries.
