Mistwood

Seat of House Mertyns

A seat deep in the misty rainwood.

The fuller tale

Mistwood sits deep in the rainwood of Cape Wrath, where the sea fogs roll inland through trees old enough to remember the children of the forest, and House Mertyns keeps a seat more heard of than seen — which its neighbors will tell you is also true of the castle itself on most mornings. It is a lordship of timber, game and solitude, its banners carrying the white owl of a house that prefers watching to being watched. The tale's wars pull its levies out of the trees like everyone else's — storm lords have no neutrality to sell — but Mistwood's real allegiance has always been to the wood, the wet, and the long views of a house that measures history in trees rather than kings.
