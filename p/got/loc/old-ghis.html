Old Ghis

Ruin of the Ghiscari Empire

The mother city of the old empire the dragons of Valyria burned five thousand years ago.

The fuller tale

Old Ghis was an empire when Valyria was a rumor of shepherds among volcanoes. The harpy city built the worldÔÇÖs first great war machine ÔÇö the lockstep legions, freeborn soldiers with tall shields and iron discipline whose memory the Unsullied are bred to counterfeit ÔÇö and planted colonies around the bay that still bears its business modelÔÇÖs name. Then the Freehold rose, and the two powers fought five wars. Ghis won none of them. Legions, courage and empire proved to be answers to every military problem except dragons; the fifth war ended with the mother city burned to ash, her walls thrown down, her fields sown with salt and sulfur, and her people yoked into the very trade they had practiced on others.
