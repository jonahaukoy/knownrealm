Ramsgate

Holdfast of the eastern shore

A small seat on the White Knife's eastern shore, sworn to White Harbor.

The fuller tale

Ramsgate keeps the eastern shore of the White Knife where the river opens toward the Bite — a small stone seat in White Harbor’s orbit, tied to the Manderlys by marriage and habit. It is the working North the songs skip: fishing fleets, river tolls, levies mustered and sent wherever the wolves point. Places like Ramsgate are why White Harbor can promise the Starks a thousand swords and mean it.

The fallen of this place — if any, they are named here. Spoilers.

No death the chronicle records has come to this ground — or none the maesters have set down.
