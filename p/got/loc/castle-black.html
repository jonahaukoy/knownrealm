Castle Black

Stronghold of the Night's Watch

The chief fortress of the Night's Watch, a grim huddle of keeps at the foot of the Wall with seven hundred feet of ice at its back.

The page and the screen

The books and show elect and kill their Lord Commander differently. On the page JonÔÇÖs election is a small political novel ÔÇö Sam quietly brokering the withdrawal of two rival commanders and springing Jon as compromise ÔÇö where the show settles it with a speech and a show of hands. The assassination diverges more deeply: book Jon is stabbed after announcing he will march on Winterfell himself, by weeping brothers led by Bowen Marsh, and the published books leave him dead on the ice with his resurrection an article of reader faith. The show gives the knives to Thorne and a boy with a grievance, and answers the question the books still hold open.
