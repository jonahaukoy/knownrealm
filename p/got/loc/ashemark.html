Ashemark

Seat of House Marbrand

The Marbrands' seat at the edge of the western hills.

The fuller tale

Ashemark holds the western hills for House Marbrand under a banner of a burning tree — a house of solid rank and better reputation, whose heir Addam rides as one of Tywin Lannister’s ablest and most decent officers, the rare westerman both armies speak well of. The seat itself pays the west’s price for the northern campaign: taken and stripped when Robb’s outriders come over the hills, one of the raids that proved the lions’ own country could burn. The Marbrands rebuild, as hill houses do, and their tree burns on the banner only.

The fallen of this place — if any, they are named here. Spoilers.

No death the chronicle records has come to this ground — or none the maesters have set down.
