White Tree

Wildling village

A tiny free folk village beyond the Wall, named for the great weirwood at its heart.

The fuller tale

Whitetree is four empty hovels around one enormous weirwood — a free folk village named for its god. The tree is the largest Jon Snow has ever seen, its carved mouth big enough to swallow a sheep, and the ashes inside it say the villagers burned their dead, which in the lands beyond the Wall is not piety but pest control. When the great ranging passes through, the village is deserted: no bodies, no signs of fight, everyone simply gone. It is the story’s quietest horror beat — the North emptying itself southward ahead of something, like a beach before the wave.
