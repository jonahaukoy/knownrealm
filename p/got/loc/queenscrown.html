Queenscrown

Abandoned holdfast

A deserted tower in a lake in the New Gift, its gilded merlons remembered in its name.

The fuller tale

Queenscrown is a round stone towerhouse on an island in a lake, reached by a hidden causeway just under the water, in the abandoned country of the New Gift. The village around it emptied generations ago as the Watch dwindled and the raids grew bolder, but the tower keeps its name from a gentler century: good Queen Alysanne stayed here on her progress north, and the villagers gilded the merlons in her honor. A crown of gold on a tower of grey, in a land with nobody left to look at it.

The story gives the empty tower one loaded night. Jon Snow shelters in it with the raiding party he has infiltrated, and Ygritte beside him, when the test he cannot pass arrives: an old man, a horse, and an order to cut a strangerÔÇÖs throat to prove himself. The tale loves a near-miss, and Queenscrown stages its cruelest: two Starks under one roof, and neither knows.
