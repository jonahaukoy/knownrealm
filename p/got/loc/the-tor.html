The Tor

Seat of House Jordayne

A seaside seat on the Sea of Dorne.

The fuller tale

The Tor stands on the Sea of Dorne where the coast bends green and mild — by Dornish standards nearly lush — and House Jordayne rules the pleasantest lordship in the principality from a seat named with desert bluntness for the hill it sits on. The Jordaynes are the salty Dornish of the coasts, more traders and readers than raiders; their lord in the tale is a man of letters whose heir is his daughter, in the Dornish way that puts inheritance before chromosomes and scandalizes the other six kingdoms. The Tor's role in the wars is the coastal seat's eternal one — ships, grain and watchfulness on the sea approaches to Sunspear — performed with the mild superiority of a house whose lands the desert never quite reached.
