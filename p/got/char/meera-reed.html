Where the page and the screen part

Book Meera is still in the greenseer's cave, brother beside her, her story paused mid-journey; everything the show gave her after — the escape, Hodor's door, the long haul south, the cold farewell from a transfigured Bran — is unwritten on the page. The books braid her deeper into the backstory instead: the Knight of the Laughing Tree tale she tells Bran stars her own father at Harrenhal, and fandom has long noted that Howland Reed is the sole living witness of the tower of joy — making the Reed children, in some theories, keepers of secrets even they may not know they hold. The show quietly dropped every strand of that, along with a fan-favorite theory or two about Meera's own parentage that the books have never dignified with confirmation.

Their journey

Where the two tellings send them to the same place the road runs whole; where they part, the screen keeps the left and the books the right.
