Where the page and the screen part

The books credit him by name with the saga's most symbolically loaded metalwork — the destruction of Ice and the forging of the two swords that become Oathkeeper and Widow's Wail, plus the gilding of the Hound's helm for its grim later career on another man's head — where the show stages the reforging as an anonymous smith under Tywin's supervision in a wordless season-opening sequence, and Tobho himself appears only in the early seasons as Gendry's master. His shop's fate diverges accordingly: book Tobho is alive and working as of the published novels, his forge busy and his secrets intact, while show Tobho simply vanishes from the story once Gendry rows away — an armorer the series no longer needed, left unmentioned and presumably hammering on. In both tellings he is that rarest of King's Landing figures, a man of genuine excellence who survives by selling to all sides and belonging to none.

Their journey

Where the two tellings send them to the same place the road runs whole; where they part, the screen keeps the left and the books the right.
