Their chapters, book by book

Oberyn's paramour, a bastard of House Uller and mother to four of his Sand Snakes ÔÇö who, in the books, is the one voice begging Dorne AGAINST the war of vengeance: she has seen where it ends.

Where the page and the screen part

No character was more completely inverted by adaptation. Book Ellaria is the voice against the war of vengeance ÔÇö her 'where does it end' speech to Doran is the books' moral summary of the entire revenge theme ÔÇö while show Ellaria murders a princess, a prince and Dorne's line of succession to start that very war, a rewrite so total that the two versions share only a name, a lover and a birthplace. The show also compresses the family tree: its Ellaria is mother-figure to all three Sand Snakes, where the books give her only the four youngest of eight, none of the three the show kept. Her show fate ÔÇö chained in the Red Keep's black cells, condemned to watch her poisoned daughter's fate at close, permanent range ÔÇö is Cersei's mirror-justice for Myrcella and has, like everything else in the arc, no book counterpart at all.

Their journey

Where the two tellings send them to the same place the road runs whole; where they part, the screen keeps the left and the books the right.
