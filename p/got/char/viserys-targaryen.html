Viserys Targaryen

House Targaryen

The exiled would-be king, last living son of the Mad King, who has dragged his little sister across the Free Cities all their lives, hawking her for an army to take him home.

A soul of House Targaryen, born at Dragonstone.

How it ends for them

On the screen. Given a golden crown (S1┬ÀE6)

The beggar king demanded his golden crown at swordpoint in Vaes Dothrak, where no blade may be drawn. Khal Drogo gave him one: molten gold, poured over his head. He was no dragon ÔÇö fire cannot kill a dragon.

On the page. A crown of molten gold (AGOT, ch. 47)

He drew steel in Vaes Dothrak, where blades are death, and demanded the crown he was promised with a sword at his sister's belly. Khal Drogo melted his medallion belt in a stewpot and crowned him with it. He was no dragon.

Viserys dies in Vaes Dothrak, the city beneath the Mother of Mountains where no blade may be drawn and no blood spilled, and both tellings savor the loophole that kills him. Drunk past caution at a feast in his sisterÔÇÖs honor, he drew his sword ÔÇö the sacrilege alone earning death ÔÇö and laid its point against DaenerysÔÇÖs belly, telling Drogo he would take her back unless the promised crown was paid. Drogo answered that he would have his golden crown, and men should look on it and tremble; his riders broke ViserysÔÇÖs sword arm and held him kneeling while the khal melted gold over the fire and poured it, molten, over his head.

The books have Daenerys watch without looking away and deliver the verdict that fire cannot kill a dragon; the show keeps the scene nearly intact, down to the awful weight of the finished crown hitting the floor. In both, the last Targaryen king-in-waiting dies without the story ever once granting him the name of king.
