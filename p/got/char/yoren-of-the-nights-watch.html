Yoren

The Night's Watch

A crook-backed wandering crow of the Night's Watch, gruff and foul-mouthed, who ranges the realm gathering recruits for the Wall from its dungeons and gutters.

A soul born at The Riverlands, sworn to The Night's Watch.

The fuller tale

Season One

Arya, shorn and renamed Arry, slips out of King's Landing in Yoren's recruit column.

Where they stand. Castle Black and King's Landing.

Season Two

Arya, marching north with Yoren's recruits, shares the road with a caged man named Jaqen H'ghar. Yoren dies defending his recruits; Arya, Gendry and Hot Pie are dragged to Harrenhal as prisoners.
