Gendry

House BaratheonThe Brotherhood Without Banners

A bull-strong blacksmith's apprentice from Flea Bottom with a stubborn streak, who has no notion of who his father was — though his black hair and blue eyes tell a story.

A soul of House Baratheon, born at King's Landing, sworn to The Brotherhood Without Banners.

The fuller tale

Season Two

Yoren dies defending his recruits; Arya, Gendry and Hot Pie are dragged to Harrenhal as prisoners.

Where they stand. Harrenhal.

Season Three

Melisandre leeches king's blood from Gendry over three names: Robb Stark. Balon Greyjoy. Joffrey Baratheon.

Where they stand. Dragonstone.

Season Seven

In Eastwatch — Still rowing — and swinging a warhammer like his father. Jorah returns cured; Gendry returns rowed; Jon plans madness: capture a wight to convince two queens.

Where they stand. Eastwatch-by-the-Sea and Dragonstone.

How it ends for them

The chronicle records no death for this soul — they are living when the tale leaves them, or their ending is a page the maesters have yet to turn.

Where the page and the screen part

The forks matter. Book Gendry is never sold, never leeched, never rowed anywhere: Melisandre's king's-blood candidate in the books is Edric Storm, Robert's acknowledged bastard, and the show folded the two boys into one — giving Gendry Edric's peril and, eventually, something like Edric's status. Book Gendry stays in the Riverlands, knighted by Beric Dondarrion, working the forge at the inn at the crossroads when Brienne's road passes through — a meeting the show never stages. And the books surround him with a darker Brotherhood than the one that sold his show-self: what he serves by the fourth book is no longer the outfit that knighted him, which is its own quiet tragedy in progress.

Their journey

Where the two tellings send them to the same place the road runs whole; where they part, the screen keeps the left and the books the right.
