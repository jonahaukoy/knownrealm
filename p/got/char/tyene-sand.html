How it ends for them

On the screen. Lost in King's Landing (S7·E3)

Captured with her mother and marched through King's Landing, she was given the same poisoned kiss Ellaria gave Myrcella — while Cersei made the mother watch, chained just out of reach, for as long as the light lasted.

The show gives her the story's most engineered death: a mirror held up to her mother's crime. Captured with Ellaria when Euron's fleet takes the Dornish party at sea, Tyene is marched through King's Landing and delivered to Cersei, whose daughter Myrcella died of Ellaria's poisoned farewell kiss. In the black cells, Cersei administers the sentence she has clearly rehearsed: the same poison, the Long Farewell, pressed to Tyene's lips with a kiss — and the antidote drunk in front of them — while mother and daughter are chained to opposite walls, close enough to see and never to touch. Ellaria's punishment is to watch it, and then to keep watching for as long as her own life is maintained; Tyene's death arrives on the poison's slow schedule, off camera, with the certainty of arithmetic. In the published books Tyene Sand is alive, en route to the Faith, her kisses so far entirely her own business.

Their chapters, book by book

Where the page and the screen part

The tellings share her surface — the septa's daughter, the poison, the smile — and split her purpose entirely. Book Tyene's infiltration of the Faith militant is unbegun on screen; show Tyene instead stabs Areo Hotah in the back during the coup and spends her last arc as a prisoner of the plot's consequences. The 'bad pussy' fight at the Water Gardens and the poisoned-blade flirtation with Bronn — cut, cure and all — are pure show invention, as is everything that follows from Euron's ambush. Her book self has never been captured, never touched her cousin's guard, and is last seen being fitted for a habit with murder in its hems.

Their journey

Where the two tellings send them to the same place the road runs whole; where they part, the screen keeps the left and the books the right.
