Grenn

The Night's Watch

A big, slow-witted, good-hearted farmer's son turned brother of the Night's Watch, one of Jon Snow's first friends at the Wall.

A soul born at The Reach, sworn to The Night's Watch.

The fuller tale

How it ends for them

On the screen. Fell holding the gate (S4·E9)

Held the inner gate of Castle Black with five brothers against a giant of the free folk. The gate held. None of the six stood up afterward; they said the words as they died.

The show gives him the death of a much grander name. When Mance’s giant, Mag the Mighty, forces the tunnel under the Wall, Grenn and five brothers are sent down to hold the inner gate — six men against a king of the giants in a corridor of ice. Jon finds them afterward: the gate held, the giant dead, and all six brothers fallen where they stood. Grenn led them in the vow as they died — the shield that guards the realms of men — and the tale offers no cleaner statement of what the Night’s Watch is for. He is alive in the published books.

Their chapters, book by book

Where the page and the screen part

Book-Grenn survives the series as published — through the Fist, the mutiny’s aftermath and the defense of Castle Black, still cheerfully enduring the old nickname, last seen going about the Watch’s business. His show death at the inner gate is an invention, and a pointed one: the screen version gathers the story’s affection for the Watch’s ordinary men into one stand and spends it all at once. Readers should also note the book’s fuller comedy of his friendship with Pyp — the pair function as a double act through three novels — where the show, with less time, sketches it in glances.

Their journey

Where the two tellings send them to the same place the road runs whole; where they part, the screen keeps the left and the books the right.
