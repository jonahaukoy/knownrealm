Where the page and the screen part

In the books Old Nan is a steady presence of the Winterfell chapters until the sack, when she is led away with the other captives toward the Dreadfort and out of the published story — her fate, like so much of the North's, an open question mark rather than a closed account. The show gave her only a pair of first-season scenes with Bran before the death of actress Margaret John, to whose memory an episode was dedicated; her storytelling function was quietly redistributed, mostly to the Reed children and to Bran's own visions. The books also credit her with the tale's densest single info-drop — the full Long Night mythology arrives through her — where the show spread that exposition across many mouths.

Their journey

Where the two tellings send them to the same place the road runs whole; where they part, the screen keeps the left and the books the right.
