How it ends for them

On the screen. Fell at Winterfell (S8┬ÀE3)

The last giant of the free folk broke the gate of Winterfell for the Starks and died against its wall, feathered with arrows and bolts, Ramsay's final shaft in his eye. With him went the giants from the world of men.

In the show the last giant dies buying Winterfell back for the Starks. At the Battle of the Bastards, Wun Wun anchors the wildling line through the slaughter, survives the arrow-storm and the crush, and when the survivors reach WinterfellÔÇÖs sealed gates he breaks them open with his own body ÔÇö shouldering through the oak and iron until the way is clear and he can no longer stand. Feathered with shafts and bolts, kneeling in the breach he made, he looks up as Ramsay Bolton puts a final arrow in his eye. Jon Snow reaches him in time to watch him fall; the death goes unavenged for approximately ninety seconds. With him, the show quietly notes, the age of giants ends. He is alive in the published books.
