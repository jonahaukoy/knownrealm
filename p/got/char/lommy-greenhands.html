How it ends for them

A dyer's apprentice bound for the Wall, too lame to walk after the ambush on Yoren's column. Polliver asked if he could walk, then put Needle through his throat and carried the sword away.

On the page. Killed on the march (ACOK, ch. 20)

The dyer's boy took a spear through his lame leg on the march and couldn't walk. When Raff the Sweetling asked if he could carry him and Lommy said yes, Raff drove the spear through his throat instead.

He dies on the march, helpless, and the manner of it is engineered to be unbearable.
