Yara Greyjoy

House Greyjoy

Balon's daughter (Asha in the books), a hard-handed reaver and ship's captain who commands ironborn as easily as her father ever did ÔÇö and everything her brother wishes he could be.

A soul of House Greyjoy, born at Pyke.
