Where the page and the screen part

The show cut Willas entire, and the amputation rearranged House Tyrell: Loras became heir (inventing the Faith's designs on Highgarden's succession), and the Willas marriage plot was rewritten as a Sansa–Loras betrothal, trading the books' poignant stranger for a familiar face and losing the joke that Sansa's dreamed-of husband is the one Tyrell nobody could caricature. His absence also thins the books' larger argument that House Tyrell's bench — heir, spare, and champion — ran deeper than any rival's, which is precisely why the books' Reach is harder to destroy in an afternoon.
