Willas Tyrell

House Tyrell

Mace's eldest son and heir to Highgarden, lamed in his first tourney: a gentle, bookish breeder of hawks and horses, wiser than most of his house (book only).

The fuller tale

Willas Tyrell is the heir to Highgarden nobody outside the books has met: the eldest of Mace's four children, lamed in his first tourney when a lance's misfortune crushed his leg, and remade by the injury into something rarer than another Reach lance — a scholar-lord in waiting. He breeds the finest hawks, hounds and horses in the Seven Kingdoms from a library chair, reads the stars, and holds no grudge against the Dornish prince whose tilt ruined the leg; he and Oberyn Martell became correspondents afterward, trading letters about horseflesh across a border their houses' songs insist is a blood feud. In a family of performers, Willas is the quiet proof that the Tyrell cleverness did not skip the male line — it just sat down.

The story deploys him as a marriage: the Tyrells' secret plan to wed Sansa Stark to Willas — sold to Sansa as rescue, and received by her as one; she daydreams of Highgarden's safety with an ache the books let land — is the scheme that panics Tywin Lannister into marrying her to Tyrion first. Willas never appears on the page for any of it; he is a future Sansa is robbed of sight unseen, which may be the most Sansa sentence in the saga. He remains at Highgarden, alive, the Reach's steadiest asset in a story that has not yet come for him.

The page and the screen

The show cut Willas entire, and the amputation rearranged House Tyrell: Loras became heir (inventing the Faith's designs on Highgarden's succession), and the Willas marriage plot was rewritten as a Sansa–Loras betrothal, trading the books' poignant stranger for a familiar face and losing the joke that Sansa's dreamed-of husband is the one Tyrell nobody could caricature. His absence also thins the books' larger argument that House Tyrell's bench — heir, spare, and champion — ran deeper than any rival's, which is precisely why the books' Reach is harder to destroy in an afternoon.
