Where the page and the screen part

The show keeps only the function: an armored figure among Joffrey's beaters, unnamed in most viewers' memories and quietly absent after the early seasons, his duties absorbed by Trant's more legible villainy. The books keep the man ÔÇö the stripped cloak, the humiliating reinstatement, the food-taster's twilight, and Jaime's withering entries on him ÔÇö because the novels are conducting a study of the Kingsguard as a decaying institution and need Boros as evidence. He is alive in both tellings as far as either goes: the published books leave him tasting Tommen's suppers with poor grace, and the show simply loses track of him, the fate of every background sworn sword the camera stops needing.

Their journey

Where the two tellings send them to the same place the road runs whole; where they part, the screen keeps the left and the books the right.
