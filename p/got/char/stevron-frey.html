Where the page and the screen part

Stevron never appears in the show, which needed only one old Frey and kept the worst one. His absence flattens a book irony the adaptation had no room for: the succession at the Twins, that endless queue of scheming heirs, exists because the decent man at its head died waiting ÔÇö and every Frey behind him was worse than the last.
