How it ends for them

The chronicle records no death for this soul — they are living when the tale leaves them, or their ending is a page the maesters have yet to turn.

Their chapters, book by book

Where the page and the screen part

Book Melessa exists at the edge of Sam's chapters — a loving presence, four children, and no scenes of her own; her defiance of Randyll is entirely the show's invention, and one of its kindest. The show also puts Heartsbane's theft and Sam's flight from Horn Hill into her orbit, whereas the books keep Sam's break with his father to the single ultimatum years before. Nothing in the published books threatens her; her house's show-fate has no page counterpart yet, leaving book Melessa mistress of an intact and dangerous Horn Hill.

Their journey

Where the two tellings send them to the same place the road runs whole; where they part, the screen keeps the left and the books the right.
