On the page. Died abed, given to the river (ASOS, ch. 46)

The Lord of Riverrun died slowly of the sickness in his belly while the war burned his rivers, calling out 'Tansy' for a wrong done long ago. Edmure fired three arrows over his funeral boat; the Blackfish's fourth wasn't needed.

In the show he dies of his long illness offscreen between seasons, and the third episode of the third season is his farewell: Riverrun gives its lord to the river in the old way, his body sent down the water in a burning boat. EdmureÔÇÖs flaming arrows fall short again and again until the Blackfish takes the bow from his hands, and the scene doubles as a portrait of the family he left ÔÇö the earnest heir who cannot quite manage, and the old soldier who does not stay to watch the shaft land.

The books tell it much the same, a chapter or two deeper: he dies abed at Riverrun in A Storm of Swords with Catelyn near, after months of poppy-dulled twilight, and the funeral boat goes down the river trailing fire while his sworn men watch from the walls. Both tellings agree on the essential mercy ÔÇö of all the lords of the Trident, Hoster Tully alone was allowed to die of nothing worse than time.
