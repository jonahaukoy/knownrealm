Lewyn Martell

A prince of Dorne in a white cloak, and Princess Elia's uncle: sworn to guard a king who was keeping his own niece and her children in the capital as hostages in all but name.

A soul of House Martell, born at Sunspear.

The fuller tale

How it ends for them

Fell on the Trident, before the story — before this story begins.

He commanded the Dornish spears at the Trident, took a wound that should have finished him, refused to leave the field, and was killed there — a man keeping a vow to an institution that had stopped deserving it years earlier, in a battle fought to defend a prince who had ruined his niece's life.

Elia and her children outlived him by weeks. What was done to them in the sack is the reason Dorne's grievance is not a diplomatic position but a wound, and it is still open a generation later when this story starts.
