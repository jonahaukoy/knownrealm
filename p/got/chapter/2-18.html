A Clash of Kings — Tyrion IV

The Dorne plot comes back out of Cersei's mouth: Pycelle is the leak, and the Grand Maester's beard and freedom both come off in a black cell.

The chapter retold

The Dorne plot Tyrion seeded comes back out through Cersei’s own mouth, proving the leak runs through Grand Maester Pycelle — the realm’s supposedly neutral healer and counselor has been Cersei’s man, and before him Tywin’s, for longer than Tyrion realized. The unmasking is quick and unglamorous: Pycelle is taken to a black cell, his beard and freedom stripped off together, his careful performance of doddering neutrality finally punctured.

It’s a small scene with outsized consequences — Tyrion has just removed his sister’s eyes and ears from the council table and replaced Pycelle’s whispers with silence, at least for now. The chapter also confirms something about how this council has always worked: nobody at that table has ever been as harmless as they perform, and Tyrion’s willingness to act on that insight, rather than merely suspect it, is what starts making him dangerous to people who underestimated him.
