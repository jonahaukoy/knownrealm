A Feast for Crows — Victarion I

The Iron Captain comes to Old Wyk among longships beyond counting; the captains weigh Asha's wit against Victarion's iron — until a sail with a red eye appears.

The chapter retold

Victarion Greyjoy, the Iron Fleet's grim and literal-minded captain, arrives at Old Wyk for the kingsmoot among more longships than the Iron Islands have gathered in living memory. The assembled captains weigh their choices — Asha's wit and popularity against Victarion's raw martial reputation — a contest that seems settled and predictable right up until a strange sail appears on the horizon, marked with a red eye, that changes the calculus for everyone watching it approach.

The chapter sets the board for the kingsmoot proper: two credible, known claimants each with real support, and the ominous late arrival of a third whose reputation (reaver, kinslayer, sorcerer by rumor) precedes him by years. Victarion's simplicity — a warrior who believes in strength as the only legitimate argument — is established here specifically so his later manipulation by his brother Euron lands as tragedy rather than surprise: he is exactly the kind of honest, blunt man a clever liar finds easiest to use.
