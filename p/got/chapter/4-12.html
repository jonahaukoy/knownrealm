A Feast for Crows ÔÇö Asha I

At Ten Towers Asha musters her uncles and captains for the kingsmoot: a woman's claim to the Seastone Chair, argued with wit, salt, and an axe.

The chapter retold

Asha Greyjoy ÔÇö introduced here by her book name, called Yara in the show ÔÇö musters her uncles and captains at Ten Towers ahead of the kingsmoot, building a coalition to press her own claim to the Seastone Chair. She argues her case with wit, sea-salt bluntness and an axe she is plainly willing to use, positioning herself as Balon's only child who ever actually loved the Iron Islands rather than merely wanting to rule them. Her uncles Victarion and Euron loom as rival claimants even before the assembly convenes ÔÇö Euron mostly absent and mysterious, having been gone from the Iron Islands for years on reaving voyages no one can fully account for.

This chapter is Asha's formal introduction as a political actor rather than merely Theon's more capable sister, and it frames the kingsmoot to come as a genuine contest rather than a formality. Her claim is unprecedented ÔÇö the ironborn have never crowned a woman ÔÇö and the chapter makes clear she knows it, campaigning not on tradition but on competence and blood loyalty to a homeland her rivals have spent their strength on: Euron on distant plunder, Victarion on obedience to a brother now dead.
