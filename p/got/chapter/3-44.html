The chapter retold

Under skies heavy with the coming storm, Sandor Clegane drives hard for the Twins with Arya Stark on his saddle, racing a wedding he has heard about only in fragments and hoping to arrive in time to trade her to her family before anyone else claims the opportunity first. He has no real plan beyond speed ÔÇö no allies at the Twins, no guarantee Catelyn Stark will even be willing to deal with the Hound of King's Landing ÔÇö just distance to cover and weather working against him the whole way.

Arya, for her part, rides with the strange vertigo of being close to a family she has not seen in what feels like a lifetime and still not trusting the man delivering her to them. The chapter is pure momentum, the book tightening its pacing as two of its major threads ÔÇö Arya's road and the northern host's return through the Twins ÔÇö race toward the same fixed point on the map without either party understanding what waits there.
