A Clash of Kings — Arya II

Gold cloaks ride out from the city with a warrant — for Gendry; Yoren's dirk and thirty ragged brothers send them back empty-handed.

The chapter retold

The Watch’s rearguard catches up with consequences of Arya’s first chapter: gold cloaks ride out of King’s Landing bearing a warrant for a fugitive smith’s apprentice — Gendry, wanted for reasons the boy himself doesn’t understand and Arya, who does, is careful never to explain. Yoren has spent a lifetime keeping recruits out of the crown’s hands and does it again here with nothing more than a knife at a throat and thirty ragged boys backing his refusal to hand anyone over.

It’s a small standoff by the war’s coming standards — no pitched battle, no famous names — but it does real work establishing what kind of protector Yoren is and what kind of secret Gendry is carrying without knowing it. Robert’s bastard son, unclaimed and unaware, is exactly the sort of loose thread Cersei’s regime wants tied off quietly, and the gold cloaks’ failure here buys him more road before that thread gets pulled again.
