The chapter retold

Chett keeps picket on the Fist of the First Men rehearsing a smaller war than the one about to arrive. He has men, he has a night picked out, and none of it accounts for what the dark beyond the firelight is about to do to his schedule. The horn sounds once, and the camp assumes rangers returning. It sounds twice, which means wildlings, and hands go to sword hilts. The third blast has not been heard by any man living, and every dog in camp starts howling before it even finishes.
