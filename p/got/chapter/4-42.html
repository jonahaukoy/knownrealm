A Feast for Crows — Alayne II

The Eyrie empties down the mountain for winter; Littlefinger unveils the next move — Harry the Heir for Alayne, and with him, one day, the Vale's knights for Sansa Stark.

The chapter retold

As winter approaches, the Eyrie empties down the mountain, its household relocating for the season, and amid the logistics Littlefinger reveals his next long-range maneuver to Sansa: he intends to marry Alayne Stone to Harrold Hardyng — Harry the Heir, next in line to the Vale should young Robert Arryn die — binding her, under her false identity, into the Vale's own succession. And beneath that plan sits another, unspoken one: that one day, once the pieces are in place, the Vale's knights might be turned toward restoring not Alayne Stone but Sansa Stark to power in the North.

The chapter is Littlefinger laying out, for the first time explicitly, just how far ahead he is thinking — using Sansa's disguise not merely as protective cover but as the raw material for an entirely new political marriage and a long-term bid to put actual military force (the Vale's knights) behind a Stark restoration he intends to personally broker and control. It's the clearest statement yet of Littlefinger's ultimate design: not simply protecting Sansa, but building her, piece by piece, into the instrument of his own ambitions.
