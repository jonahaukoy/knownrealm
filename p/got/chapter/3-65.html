A Storm of Swords — Jon VIII

Back from the wildlings, Jon is called turncloak by Janos Slynt and Alliser Thorne; his reward for holding the Wall is an arrest, and worse planned.

The chapter retold

Jon Snow returns to Castle Black from his time among the wildlings expecting, at minimum, to be believed. Instead Ser Alliser Thorne and Janos Slynt — installed in positions of authority at the Wall in Jon's absence — brand him a turncloak, seizing on his time with Mance Rayder's people as proof of treason rather than the intelligence-gathering it actually was, regardless of the warning about an imminent assault that Jon has just delivered at cost to himself.

The chapter is a bitter homecoming: Jon survived climbing the Wall, fell in love, broke his vows, and rode back wounded specifically to warn his brothers of the army bearing down on them, and his reward is arrest and the promise of worse to come. It sharpens the internal politics of the Watch into as real a threat to Jon as anything north of the ice — an institution that punishes the very loyalty it claims to demand.
