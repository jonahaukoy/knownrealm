A Clash of Kings — Jon VIII

In the mountains Qhorin makes Jon keep his promise: Ghost's teeth and Jon's sword end the Halfhand, and a turncloak walks into Mance Rayder's camp.

The chapter retold

In the mountains, Qhorin Halfhand forces Jon to complete the hardest order he’s ever been given — the two of them stage a fight in front of the wildlings holding them, ending with Ghost’s teeth and Jon’s sword bringing Qhorin down, exactly as the Halfhand instructed. It is a death engineered on purpose, sacrificed deliberately to make Jon’s supposed defection to Mance Rayder’s cause look real to the wildlings watching.

Jon walks into Mance’s camp afterward carrying clean hands by the letter of his orders and a black heart by every other measure — he has just killed a man he respected, on that man’s own command, to sell a lie about his own loyalties. Qhorin Halfhand dies exactly as he intended to, which does nothing to make the act easier for the man who had to do the killing. It’s one of the book’s clearest illustrations of what the Watch actually asks of the men who serve it — not just their lives, but sometimes their honor, spent deliberately for a larger and colder purpose.
