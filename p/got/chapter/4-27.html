The chapter retold

In Braavos, Sam's simmering resentment of Dareon boils over into an actual brawl ÔÇö a fight about duty, desertion and the vows both of them supposedly swore, fought with fists rather than words for once. Bloodied and humiliated, Sam returns to find Maester Aemon suddenly, briefly awake and lucid, asking urgent questions about dragons ÔÇö and about a name that catches in Sam's throat to hear a blind, dying old man say with such longing: Daenerys.

The scene is a quiet gut-punch disguised as a minor domestic squabble: Sam's physical fight with Dareon (whom the reader, thanks to Arya's earlier chapter, may already suspect is a dead man walking) is almost comic in its clumsiness, but it's staged directly against Aemon's sudden, lucid fixation on Daenerys ÔÇö the last Targaryen maester's dying preoccupation with the last Targaryen queen he will never meet. It's the clearest signal yet that Aemon's storyline is racing toward its end, and that whatever peace he finds will be bound up entirely in that name.
