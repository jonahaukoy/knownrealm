A Storm of Swords — Arya XIII

At the crossroads inn Polliver is wearing Needle: the Hound takes his wine, Arya takes her sword back, and leaves Sandor dying under a tree — off her list, unlisted.

The chapter retold

At a crossroads inn, Arya Stark finds Polliver — one of the men who tormented her at Harrenhal in the previous book — wearing Needle, the sword her father gave her that she thought lost for good. Sandor Clegane, weakened and feverish but still capable, occupies the room by taking Polliver's wine while Arya, in a scene the book plays with cold, exact patience, reclaims her sword by killing the man wearing it.

She does not stop there: the Tickler, another of Polliver's company and a man whose name she has recited on her list for chapters, gets the same question he once asked his own victims — is there gold in the village — before Arya kills him too, calm in a way that is more disturbing than rage would be. She leaves Sandor Clegane dying under a tree afterward, choosing to walk away rather than finish him herself, taking him off her list not through forgiveness but through indifference — he is simply no longer worth the effort of hating.
