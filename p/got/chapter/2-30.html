A Clash of Kings — Tyrion VII

Cersei springs her trap on the wrong whore: Alayaya pays for Shae, and Tyrion promises his sister a debt he means to collect in kind.

The chapter retold

Cersei springs an elaborate trap meant to catch Tyrion through his mistress Shae, and catches the wrong woman entirely — Alayaya, a prostitute recruited to stand in as a decoy, is beaten and humiliated in Shae’s place while the real target stays safely hidden. Tyrion arrives too late to prevent it and finds Alayaya paying, in blood and bruises, for a lie he built to protect someone he loves.

His response is cold and specific: a promise to his sister that the debt will be collected in kind, cruelty answered with cruelty rather than diplomacy. It’s a rare moment of Tyrion abandoning cleverness for something closer to Lannister instinct, and it marks a real hardening in the siblings’ war — no longer just political rivals, but two people now actively hunting for ways to hurt each other through the people they care about.
