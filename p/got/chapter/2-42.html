A Clash of Kings — Tyrion IX

Myrcella sails for Dorne and the city's rage boils over: the mob tears the High Septon apart, nearly takes Sansa, and the Hound earns his pay.

The chapter retold

Myrcella sails for Dorne to seal the betrothal Tyrion arranged chapters ago, and the city she leaves behind erupts the moment she’s gone. Starving, furious, and out of patience with a court that feasts while Flea Bottom goes hungry, a mob turns on the High Septon during a procession and tears him apart with bare hands — the closest thing King’s Landing has to a spiritual authority, destroyed by the very people his prayers failed to feed.

The riot nearly claims Sansa too, caught in the open when the crowd turns feral, and it’s the Hound — grim, efficient, uninterested in praise — who cuts a path through the mob and earns his pay in blood rather than ceremony. The chapter is the clearest picture yet of how close King’s Landing sits to total collapse: not armies at the gates but its own starving population, one procession away from tearing the city apart from the inside.
