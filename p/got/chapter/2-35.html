A Clash of Kings — Jon IV

The Watch camps on the Fist of the First Men; under the ashes Ghost noses out an old cloak wrapped around dragonglass.

The chapter retold

The Watch’s great ranging finally reaches the Fist of the First Men, an ancient ringfort where Mormont decides to make camp and take stock. Under a pile of ashes, Ghost noses out something that makes Sam’s blood run cold — an old cloak wrapped carefully around a stash of dragonglass daggers, hidden by hands unknown for a purpose everyone can guess at.

It’s a small, quiet discovery that lands like a thunderclap once its implications sink in: someone, at some point, thought it worth hiding weapons specifically effective against the Others out here in the middle of nowhere. The Fist itself, ancient and defensible, becomes the ranging’s forward base — a decision that will look wiser and then far more dangerous as the book’s northern plot builds toward its climax.
