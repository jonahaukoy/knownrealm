The chapter retold

Arya Stark finally attempts what the Brotherhood Without Banners' slow, diverted road never let her have ÔÇö an escape on her own terms ÔÇö and runs directly into someone worse. Sandor Clegane, the Hound, catches her fleeing and takes her for his own purpose: not cruelty exactly, but a transaction, intending to sell her to her mother at the Twins for a ransom he judges will be considerably larger than whatever the Brotherhood was going to collect.

It is a bitter trade for Arya ÔÇö out of outlaw captivity and into the hands of a man whose reputation she has feared since King's Landing ÔÇö but also, unmistakably, the last unbroken thread connecting her to the family she has spent two books trying to reach. The Hound's plan puts them both, unknowingly, on a collision course with the very worst night the war has left in store for the Starks.
