A Storm of Swords — Samwell V

Maester Aemon calls the question at last: Sam's quiet lies bring Cotter Pyke and Denys Mallister to withdraw in favor of a compromise no one saw coming.

The chapter retold

Maester Aemon, blind and ancient but still the Watch's moral center, finally calls the long-delayed vote for Lord Commander to order. The frontrunners — Cotter Pyke of Eastwatch and Denys Mallister of the Shadow Tower — have deadlocked the contest between them for chapters, each man's supporters refusing to yield ground to the other's candidate.

Samwell Tarly's quiet lobbying, built on nothing more dramatic than conversation and patience, persuades both men to withdraw in favor of a compromise candidate neither faction saw coming — a resolution achieved entirely through Sam's unglamorous political work rather than any battle. The chapter rewards the kind of courage the previous chapter reframed for the reader, proving that Sam's real gift to the Watch was never going to be swordplay.

This movement: Gold, Ice, and the Hanged Woman

  • Jaime frees his brother; Tyrion detours through the Tower of the Hand: Shae in the bed, Tywin on the privy, a crossbow for both.
  • Lysa Arryn flies from the Moon Door screaming about tears and dark words; Littlefinger blames a singer.
  • Jon Snow is elected Lord Commander of the Night's Watch on Sam's clever tongue.
  • Epilogue at the Twins: the Brotherhood hangs Freys for a hooded woman whose throat gapes — Lady Stoneheart remembers.
