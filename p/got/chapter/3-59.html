A Storm of Swords — Tyrion VII

Oberyn Martell talks poisons and old murders with Tyrion over wine: Dorne remembers Elia, and the Viper has come north for more than a wedding.

The chapter retold

Over wine, Oberyn Martell and Tyrion Lannister conduct a conversation that is really an interrogation dressed up as pleasant company — poisons, old murders, the fine art of getting away with both, all discussed with a connoisseur's relish that never quite disguises what Oberyn is actually circling. He brings up Elia Martell, his sister, dead at the Sack of King's Landing along with her children, and makes plain that Dorne's grievance is not diplomatic posturing; it is personal, and it has been waiting sixteen years for an opening.

Tyrion, who had no hand in Elia's death and knows it, still finds himself treated as an extension of the family that ordered it — a reminder that in this story, blood answers for blood regardless of individual guilt. The chapter deepens Oberyn from a colorful new arrival into a genuine threat with a target list, and confirms that whatever he has actually come to King's Landing to do, watching a royal wedding is only ever going to be part of it.
