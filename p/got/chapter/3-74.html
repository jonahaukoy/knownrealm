The chapter retold

In the aftermath of Stannis's rout of the wildling army, Jon Snow moves among the captured free folk counting a cost that victory does not erase ÔÇö men, women and children he lived among for a season, now prisoners of the side he nominally serves, their cause broken not because it was wrong but because Stannis had the larger army and no interest in negotiation once the advantage was his.

Melisandre watches Jon throughout with an attention that has nothing casual in it, the kind of focus the book has already taught readers to associate with her fires and her plans for king's blood. The chapter leaves Jon in an uncomfortable position ÔÇö victorious by technicality, morally unsettled by what victory required, and newly, uncomfortably visible to a woman whose interest in him is unlikely to be simple curiosity.
