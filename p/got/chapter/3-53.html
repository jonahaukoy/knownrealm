The chapter retold

Arya Stark arrives at the Twins just as the slaughter spills out of the hall and into the yards beyond it, close enough to hear what is happening and too late by minutes to change any of it. The Hound, understanding faster than she does what they have ridden into, knocks her senseless with the flat of his axe rather than let her run toward a massacre that would swallow her too, dragging her unconscious body clear of the carnage while the camp burns and screams behind them.

That night, in the strange half-dream state of her wolf-bond, Arya runs the riverbank as Nymeria and finds a body drifting in the water ÔÇö unidentified to her in the moment, though the reader has every reason to suspect whose it is. The chapter denies Arya both the reunion she spent the book chasing and even the clean grief of confirmed loss, leaving her instead with a half-glimpsed horror and a captor who, however brutal his methods, has just saved her life for reasons that are no longer entirely mercenary.
