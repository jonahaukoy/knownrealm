A Storm of Swords — Catelyn IV

Robb plans his way back north through the Twins: a pardon for the Freys, Edmure for a bride, and a wedding to mend what a wedding broke.

The chapter retold

With his war seemingly won in the field but his alliances in ruins after his own marriage, Robb Stark turns to diplomacy to repair what victory could not fix: a march north back through the Twins, with Roose Bolton sent ahead to negotiate a pardon from Walder Frey for the broken betrothal, and Edmure Tully — Catelyn's brother, Riverrun's heir — offered up as a substitute bridegroom to satisfy the insult with a different Stark-adjacent match.

It is a plan built on the assumption that Walder Frey's pride can be bought off with a lesser prize than the one he was originally promised, and the chapter's quiet dramatic irony is that everyone involved treats this as a solvable logistics problem rather than a mortal insult that a man like Frey does not simply let go. Catelyn's unease is present but muted; the reader, by this point in the book, has more reason than she does to distrust the plan's premise entirely.
