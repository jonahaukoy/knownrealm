The chapter retold

Maimed, fevered and half out of his mind, Jaime Lannister is dragged in Vargo Hoat's train to Harrenhal, the stump of his sword hand rotting faster than his captors care to notice. Qyburn ÔÇö a maester stripped of his chain for experiments the Citadel would not sanction ÔÇö takes an interest in keeping him alive that has nothing to do with mercy and everything to do with curiosity, and between competent care and sheer spite, Jaime survives the infection that should have killed him.

What pulls him back from the edge is not hope so much as its opposite: a determination to live precisely so he can be avenged on everyone who let this happen to him, starting with Vargo Hoat. The chapter marks the low point of Jaime's physical ordeal and, in the same breath, the beginning of his slow reconstruction ÔÇö a man forced to discover what he is worth without the sword hand that used to answer every question about his identity for him.
