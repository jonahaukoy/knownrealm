A Storm of Swords — Samwell IV

The election for Lord Commander stalls while Janos Slynt gathers votes; Sam the Slayer discovers that courage comes in more shapes than swords.

The chapter retold

At Castle Black, the election for the Night's Watch's new Lord Commander bogs down in factional stalemate, with Janos Slynt — disgraced former commander of the City Watch, no friend to Jon Snow — working the room for votes with a politician's shameless persistence despite having no real qualification for the post beyond ambition.

Samwell Tarly, watching the process unfold, discovers that courage in this contest looks nothing like the swordplay he has spent the book being praised for — it looks like speaking up in rooms that intimidate him, maneuvering quietly against a bully with words instead of dragonglass. The chapter reframes the entire idea of heroism the book has been building around Sam, setting up the more consequential political courage he shows in the chapters immediately following.
