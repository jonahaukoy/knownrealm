The chapter retold

In Astapor's training yards, the slaver Kraznys mo Nakloz shows off his Unsullied to Daenerys Targaryen with a salesman's cruelty, narrating their conditioning in fluent, obscene Valyrian and trusting his translator, the slave girl Missandei, to soften every insult for his foreign customer's ears. Dany listens to every word of the unsoftened original ÔÇö she speaks High Valyrian fluently, a fact Kraznys has not bothered to consider possible in a Westerosi girl ÔÇö and lets him keep insulting her, banking the information and the humiliation both for later use.

The scene is a masterclass in underestimation: Kraznys sees a naive foreign girl haggling for soldiers; the reader sees a queen assembling both an army and a grievance list in the same conversation. Astapor's Unsullied ÔÇö bred to feel nothing, obey absolutely, and die without complaint ÔÇö are being appraised by the one buyer in the yard who intends to make that obedience serve something other than slavery, though neither Kraznys nor his translator understands that yet.
