A Clash of Kings — Sansa II

A note leads Sansa to the godswood by night: Ser Dontos, her Florian now, promises to see her home to Winterfell — for the Mother's mercy.

The chapter retold

A folded note leads Sansa into the godswood by night, where the man she thinks of as her Florian keeps his promise from chapter three: Ser Dontos, drunk fool turned secret conspirator, swears he will see her safely home to Winterfell. He asks for nothing but her trust and the Mother’s mercy, a strange, fumbling little scene that reads more like desperation than competence.

The chapter matters less for what happens in it than for what it sets in motion — Sansa’s first real hope of escape since Ned’s death, offered by the least likely rescuer imaginable. Whether Dontos can actually deliver on a promise made by a disgraced, frightened man is very much an open question, but for Sansa, starved of any exit at all, even a fragile one is worth the risk of trusting him.
