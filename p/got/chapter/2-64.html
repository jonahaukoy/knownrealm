A Clash of Kings — Daenerys V

Qarth exhausted, Daenerys takes stock at the docks: three ships bought with promises, and a course set east before south — home by the long way.

The chapter retold

In the aftermath at Qarth, Daenerys takes stock of what her visit has actually cost and gained — three ships, purchased with promises rather than gold, and a war of assassins and rivals she’s just narrowly escaped. She sets a course east rather than the direct route south toward Westeros, choosing the long way home over the fast one, aware that arriving unprepared would be its own kind of defeat.

It’s a quiet, deliberate chapter of consolidation after the House of the Undying’s chaos — Daenerys weighing patience against urgency and choosing patience, a discipline that will define much of her arc going forward. The ships are small, but they’re real, tangible progress after a stretch of the book that offered her mostly visions and near-disasters.

This movement: Valar Morghulis

  • Tywin is Hand and savior; the Tyrells bring a queen and a rose-colored future; Sansa is quietly set aside.
  • Winterfell is sacked — by Bolton men under Ramsay's flayed banner, not the ironborn; Luwin dies at the heart tree.
  • Bran and Rickon split shadows: Rickon east with Osha, Bran north with the Reeds toward the Wall.
  • The Fist of the First Men: three horn blasts in the dark, and the dead come with the snow.
