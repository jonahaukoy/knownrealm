A Storm of Swords — Arya V

In the hollow hill the brotherhood tries the Hound before Beric Dondarrion: trial by combat against a flaming sword — Sandor walks free, and a dead man is kissed back to life for the sixth time.

The chapter retold

The Brotherhood Without Banners puts Sandor Clegane on trial inside their hollow-hill hideout, presided over by Beric Dondarrion with the gravity of men who genuinely believe their makeshift justice matters. Accused of murders committed under Gregor's banner and his own reputation as the Hound, Sandor demands trial by combat rather than accept a verdict from outlaws — and gets it, against Beric himself, whose sword burns with a fire the flaming blade's owner treats as unremarkable.

Sandor wins, walks free of the charge, and in doing so witnesses something that unsettles even him: Thoros of Myr kneels over Beric's body afterward and kisses him back from death for the sixth time, a resurrection performed so often it has become almost routine to the men who travel with him. The chapter cements the Lightning Lord's motif of stubborn, diminishing returns from death — each revival costs Beric a little more of himself — while giving Arya, watching, one more reason to distrust and half-admire the man holding court over her own uncertain fate.
