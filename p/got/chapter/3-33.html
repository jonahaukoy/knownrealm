A Storm of Swords — Tyrion IV

The Red Viper of Dorne arrives for the wedding with a paramour, a hundred spears, and sixteen years of questions about his sister Elia.

The chapter retold

The Red Viper of Dorne arrives in King's Landing for Joffrey's wedding trailing exactly the kind of entrance Tyrion expected and feared: a paramour, Ellaria Sand, on his arm; a hundred Dornish spears at his back; and sixteen years of unspoken accusation about what happened to his sister Elia Martell during the Sack of King's Landing riding along with the baggage. Oberyn makes no secret that he has come north for more than a royal marriage, and Tyrion — tasked with managing every other awkward wedding arrangement in the city — now has to manage a man whose grievance against House Lannister predates his own birth.

The chapter sets Dorne's long memory against the capital's short one: everyone else at court has moved on from Robert's Rebellion as ancient history, and Oberyn's arrival is a reminder that for the people who actually lost family in it, nothing has been settled, only postponed. His presence at the wedding festivities to come is now a live threat the reader can feel ticking under every scene that follows.
