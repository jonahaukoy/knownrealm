A Game of Thrones — Catelyn VII

The Eyrie's champion Ser Vardis Egen fights Bronn for the gods' verdict — and follows his shield through the Moon Door.

The chapter retold

The Eyrie's champion, Ser Vardis Egen, is a seasoned, heavily armored knight facing a sellsword in leather — by every visible measure a mismatch in Vardis's favor. Bronn wins anyway, using patience and mobility against Vardis's weight and pride, tiring him out and finding the gaps in his armor rather than trying to overpower him outright, until Vardis finally goes down and, per the Eyrie's particular flair for justice, follows his own shield through the Moon Door to the long fall below.

The combat is a small master class in how this book treats martial reputation versus practical skill — the celebrated knight loses to the man willing to fight ugly, a pattern the series returns to often enough that it starts to feel like a rule rather than an upset. Tyrion walks free from a trial that had never really been about guilt or innocence, but the victory buys him only his life, not his family's forgiveness for having been imprisoned by a Tully in the first place — a debt Casterly Rock is already counting.
