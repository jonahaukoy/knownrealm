The fuller tale

A sister city of old Valyria, lost in the same Doom that claimed Oros and a dozen others, Tyria’s ruin sits silent through the Dance of the Dragons exactly as it sits silent through every other Westerosi war — a monument to a catastrophe no dragon since has come close to matching.

The fallen of this place — if any, they are named here. Spoilers.
