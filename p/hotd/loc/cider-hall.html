Seat of House Fossoway

The apple-sigiled Fossoways' seat at the meeting of the Mander and the Cockleswhent.

The fuller tale

The apple-sigiled Fossoways’ seat where the Mander meets the Cockleswhent sends its share of the Reach’s levies behind Ormund Hightower’s green banner, one more orchard castle whose Dance-era war is entirely a matter of other people’s battles.

The fallen of this place — if any, they are named here. Spoilers.
