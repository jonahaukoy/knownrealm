Seat of House Meadows

A green seat at the eastern edge of the Reach.

The fuller tale

The Meadows family’s green seat at the eastern edge of the Reach contributes its levies to Ormund Hightower’s host along with its neighbors, its own Dance-era history no more eventful than any other minor Reach castle asked to send men and not much else.
