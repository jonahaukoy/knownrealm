Selhorys

River town of the Rhoyne

A walled river town on the Rhoyne, tributary to Volantis.

The fuller tale

This walled river town on the Rhoyne, tributary to Volantis, has no part in the Dance of the Dragons at all — its politics belong entirely to the Free Cities’ own rivalries, and a Targaryen succession war on the far side of the narrow sea is simply not Selhorys’s business.

The fallen of this place — if any, they are named here. Spoilers.
