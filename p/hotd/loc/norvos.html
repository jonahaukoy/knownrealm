Norvos

City of the bearded priests

A hill city of bells and bearded priests above the Noyne.

The fuller tale

Norvos, ruled by its bearded priests and timed by its three great bells, takes no part in the Dance of the Dragons — the city’s wars, such as they are, run to old rivalries with Qohor over the forest between them, and a Westerosi succession dispute four hundred leagues west barely disturbs the rhythm the bells keep for the bearded fathers’ flock.

The fallen of this place — if any, they are named here. Spoilers.

No death the chronicle records has come to this ground — or none the maesters have set down.
