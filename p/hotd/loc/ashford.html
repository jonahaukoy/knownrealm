Ashford

Seat of House Ashford

A castle and market town on the Cockleswhent, once host to a famous tourney.

The fuller tale

A castle and market town famous for a tourney a generation removed from the Dance, Ashford’s role in the civil war itself is quieter — supplying and mustering for the green host gathering to the east, its own gates untroubled by siege or dragonfire.
