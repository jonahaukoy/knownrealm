The fuller tale

A grey seat gnawed at by the Bite, held by House Locke, Oldcastle contributes nothing more to the Dance of the Dragons than any other minor northern holdfast — a name on Cregan Stark’s muster roll, nothing more, nothing less.

The fallen of this place — if any, they are named here. Spoilers.
