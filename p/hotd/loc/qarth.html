Qarth

The greatest city that ever was or will be

The queen of cities on the Jade Gates, ringed by triple walls — so its merchants never tire of saying.

The fuller tale

Qarth’s merchants, who never tire of reminding visitors theirs is the greatest city that ever was or will be, have considerably more pressing trade concerns along the Jade Gates than a succession war among a lesser house of dragonlords on the far side of the world — the Dance is, from behind Qarth’s triple walls, simply not a story anyone there is telling.

The fallen of this place — if any, they are named here. Spoilers.

No death the chronicle records has come to this ground — or none the maesters have set down.
