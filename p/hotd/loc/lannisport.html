Lannisport

City of the Lannisters

A walled port city in the shadow of the Rock, home of the Lannister fleet.

The fuller tale

The walled port beneath the Rock serves the greens as the Lannister fleet’s home harbor through the Dance, its ships and its gold both committed to Aegon II’s cause from the war’s first days — and its shore, like the Rock’s own, spends the war fending off ironborn raiders taking advantage of a west coast that has more urgent business than chasing longships.

In the first season

The Lannister fleet rides at anchor here while the lions do their season's fighting on land.

The fallen of this place — if any, they are named here. Spoilers.

No death the chronicle records has come to this ground — or none the maesters have set down.
