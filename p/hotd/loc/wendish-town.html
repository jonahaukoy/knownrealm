Wendish Town

River village

A small riverside town in the Mallister lands — the kind of place armies burn on their way somewhere else.

The fuller tale

A small river town in the Mallister lands, Wendish Town has no army and no dragon and consequently no real say in the Dance of the Dragons — riverlands towns like this one supply the marching hosts that pass through and hope, as riverlands towns always hope in wartime, to still be standing when the armies are done needing them.

The fallen of this place — if any, they are named here. Spoilers.

No death the chronicle records has come to this ground — or none the maesters have set down.
