Gulltown

Port city of the Vale

The Vale's harbor city, held by House Grafton for the Arryns.

The fuller tale

The Vale’s harbor city, held for the Arryns by House Grafton, matters to the Dance mainly as a port a black-leaning Vale can use to move men and grain to wherever Rhaenyra’s cause needs them — a quiet contributor to a war fought, for the Vale, almost entirely at a comfortable distance.

In the first season

The Vale's port stays quiet — Lysa Arryn keeps her knights home no matter who writes to her.
