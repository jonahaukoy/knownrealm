The Shadow Tower

Western castle of the Watch

Westernmost manned castle of the Night's Watch, guarding the mountain end of the Wall.

The fuller tale

The westernmost castle of the Night’s Watch keeps its lonely post at the mountain end of the Wall through the Dance untouched and largely unremarked, its garrison too small and too far from any king’s road to matter to either color. Its war, as ever, is with the wildling passes it watches, not the dynasty tearing itself apart four hundred leagues south.

The fallen of this place — if any, they are named here. Spoilers.

No death the chronicle records has come to this ground — or none the maesters have set down.
