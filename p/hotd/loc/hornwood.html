Hornwood

Seat of House Hornwood

A modest forest seat between the Dreadfort and White Harbor.

The fuller tale

A modest forest seat between larger, more storied castles, Hornwood sends what men Winterfell asks for and no more — its role in the Dance, like most of the North’s, is patience, not battle, and the war ends before that patience is much tested.
