Vaes Dothrak

City of the horselords

The Dothraki's only city — no walls, no buildings but stolen monuments, watched over by the Mother of Mountains.

The fuller tale

No khal or khaleesi has any reason to care who sits the Iron Throne, and Vaes Dothrak’s walless sprawl of stolen monuments hears essentially nothing of the Dance of the Dragons — a Targaryen family quarrel over a chair the Dothraki have never wanted and would never ride toward is precisely the kind of Westerosi news that dies somewhere on the long road east.

In the first season

Daenerys eats the stallion's heart here and the crones proclaim her son. Viserys demands his crown at swordpoint — and gets one of molten gold.

The fallen of this place — if any, they are named here. Spoilers.

No death the chronicle records has come to this ground — or none the maesters have set down.
