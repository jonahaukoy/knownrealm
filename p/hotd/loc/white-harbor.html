White Harbor

City of House Manderly

The North's only true city, a clean white port at the mouth of the White Knife, held by House Manderly for the Starks.

The fuller tale

The North’s only true city keeps its harbor busy through the Dance mostly with its own trade — the Manderlys are loyal Stark bannermen and would answer the call south if Winterfell issued it in earnest, but the North’s war, when it finally comes, is fought with a marching host, not a fleet, and White Harbor’s ships stay largely in port for the whole affair.

In the first season

Never shown this season, but its lords answer when Robb Stark calls the banners of the North.

The fallen of this place — if any, they are named here. Spoilers.

No death the chronicle records has come to this ground — or none the maesters have set down.
