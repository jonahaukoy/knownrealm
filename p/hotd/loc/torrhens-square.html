Torrhen's Square

Seat of House Tallhart

A square towered keep named for the King Who Knelt.

The fuller tale

Named for the King Who Knelt, Torrhen’s Square keeps its own household quarrel small during the Dance of the Dragons — a northern castle a very long way from a southern throne, sworn to wait on Winterfell and, this once, actually rewarded for the patience.

The fallen of this place — if any, they are named here. Spoilers.

No death the chronicle records has come to this ground — or none the maesters have set down.
