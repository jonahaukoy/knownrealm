Bhonash

Waystation of the east

A dusty town on the caravan roads north of Slaver's Bay.

The fuller tale

A dusty waystation on the caravan roads north of Slaver’s Bay, Bhonash exists to service trade between cities that have never heard of Rhaenyra or Aegon II and, during the years of the Dance, have no particular reason to start.

The fallen of this place — if any, they are named here. Spoilers.

No death the chronicle records has come to this ground — or none the maesters have set down.
