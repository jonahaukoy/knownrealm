The Bloody Gate

Gate of the Vale

The narrow pass where the Knight of the Gate asks who would enter the Vale — and armies die asking.

The fuller tale

The Vale’s famous chokepoint asks its old question — who would enter the Vale? — of exactly nobody during the Dance of the Dragons; with Jeyne Arryn firmly in Rhaenyra’s camp and no green army foolish enough to try the mountain road, the Bloody Gate spends the whole war doing what it does best, which is standing there being unconquerable.

The fallen of this place — if any, they are named here. Spoilers.

No death the chronicle records has come to this ground — or none the maesters have set down.
