The fuller tale

The Vaiths’ river seat amid the red sands sits out the Dance along with the rest of the princedom, its remove from the war total enough that its own Dance-era history amounts to little more than distant rumor of dragons burning each other far to the north.

The fallen of this place — if any, they are named here. Spoilers.
