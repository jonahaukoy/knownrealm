Volon Therys

River town of the Rhoyne

A river town on the lower Rhoyne, under the sway of Volantis.

The fuller tale

Under the sway of Volantis on the lower Rhoyne, Volon Therys has no stake whatsoever in a Targaryen civil war being fought a continent away — its own concerns are the Rhoyne’s trade and Volantis’s politics, both entirely indifferent to which color currently holds King’s Landing.
