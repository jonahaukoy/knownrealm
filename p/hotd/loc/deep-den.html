Seat of House Lydden

A den-like seat in the hills along the goldroad.

The fuller tale

The Lyddens’ den-like seat along the goldroad follows the rest of the westerlands into the green camp, its part in the Dance limited to men and coin sent toward a war it never has to host on its own doorstep.

The fallen of this place — if any, they are named here. Spoilers.
