Alyn of Hull

House Velaryon

A shipwright's son of Hull with Velaryon silver in his hair and no name to hang it on — a sailor of the Sea Snake's fleet whom the Sea Snake watches very carefully.

A soul of House Velaryon, born at Driftmark.

The fuller tale

Season Two

In Regent — Saved his father's life and wants nothing for it, which is the problem. On Driftmark, Alyn of Hull — the sailor who pulled the Sea Snake from the waves — is owed more than wages, and Corlys knows it.

Where they stand. Driftmark.

Their story

A shipwright's son from Hull, on Driftmark's own shores, Alyn carries Velaryon-silver hair and a face that half the court privately suspects belongs to a bastard branch of the family — an unspoken parentage that follows him through a career as one more capable sailor in the Sea Snake's enormous fleet.

Corlys Velaryon, whatever he may privately know or suspect about Alyn's paternity, watches the young sailor's career with an attentiveness that goes well beyond ordinary interest in a talented crewman — the kind of attention a man pays when he is quietly deciding what a bastard's blood might yet be worth to a family that has just lost so many of its legitimate heirs.

How it ends for them

The chronicle records no death for this soul — they are living when the tale leaves them, or their ending is a page the maesters have yet to turn.

Their chapters, book by book

Where the page and the screen part

Alyn's story is developed at greater length in Fire & Blood, which follows his rise through Corlys's fleet in more explicit detail across the war's naval campaigns; the show's current seasons have introduced him but have not yet dramatized the fuller arc the book affords him.

Their journey

Where the two tellings send them to the same place the road runs whole; where they part, the screen keeps the left and the books the right.
