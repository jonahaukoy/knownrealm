Willem Blackwood

The Blacks — the Queen's PartyHouse Blackwood

A young lord of Raventree Hall with nine hundred years of Bracken grievances and a keen eye for which dragon settles them fastest. Kneels early, kills eagerly.

The fuller tale

A young lord of Raventree Hall, Willem Blackwood inherits not just his seat but nine hundred years of accumulated grievance against the neighboring Brackens — a rivalry so old and so thoroughly institutionalized that it functions, in the riverlands, as something close to a second religion.

The Dance offers him something the Blackwood-Bracken feud has never previously had access to: dragons. Willem kneels to Rhaenyra's cause early and enthusiastically, with a keen and entirely unsentimental eye toward which side's dragons will most efficiently settle nine centuries of scores against his hereditary enemies.

The page and the screen

Willem Blackwood exists only in the show's telling; Fire & Blood's own account of the riverlands' Blackwood-Bracken rivalry during the Dance does not single out this specific figure by name, making him one of the series' original creations built to dramatize a feud the book treats more generally.
