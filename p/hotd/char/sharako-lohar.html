The fuller tale

Their story

Admiral of the Triarchy's combined fleets at the Stepstones, Sharako Lohar commands some ninety warships and a set of personal habits the show takes evident relish in making memorable — a Lysene naval officer whose competence and eccentricity are, in roughly equal measure, the reason both the greens and the Sea Snake's blockade have to take him seriously.

Courted by the greens specifically for his fleet's ability to break Corlys Velaryon's stranglehold on the narrow sea's shipping lanes, Sharako represents the war's Essos-facing dimension — a reminder that the Dance, however Westerosi its causes, is fought partly with foreign ships and foreign admirals whose loyalty is available to the highest or most persuasive bidder.

How it ends for them

The chronicle records no death for this soul — they are living when the tale leaves them, or their ending is a page the maesters have yet to turn.

Their chapters, book by book

Where the page and the screen part

Sharako Lohar is a show-original figure with no direct named counterpart in Fire & Blood's account of the Triarchy's naval forces at the Stepstones, one of several places where the series has invented a specific personality to dramatize a role the book treats more generically as 'the Triarchy's fleet.'
