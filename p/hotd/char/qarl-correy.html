Where the page and the screen part

This is one of the sharpest divergences in the whole saga's cast: the show's Qarl Correy is Laenor's rescuer, arranging an escape from a marriage and a war that both give the real Laenor no future in Westeros. Fire & Blood's Qarl Correy is Laenor's killer, cutting him down at a fair in Spicetown for reasons ÔÇö jealousy, a bought blade, a lovers' quarrel ÔÇö the sources argue over without ever settling. Notably, neither telling records what ultimately becomes of Qarl himself; he vanishes from both versions of the history the moment his role in Laenor's story is finished.
