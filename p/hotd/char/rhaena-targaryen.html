Their chapters, book by book

Where the page and the screen part

The show and the book largely agree on Rhaena's circumstances and role, though the show, still airing, has more time yet to develop a resolution to her dragonless status than the book's already-complete account, which resolves the question definitively in chapters beyond where the series has currently reached.

Their journey

Where the two tellings send them to the same place the road runs whole; where they part, the screen keeps the left and the books the right.
