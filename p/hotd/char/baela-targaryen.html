Where the page and the screen part

Baela's characterization is broadly consistent between the show and Fire & Blood, both presenting her as spirited and capable, though the show's aired seasons have given her considerably more direct screen presence and dialogue than the book's more distant, chronicle-style treatment of the era's younger dragonriders.
