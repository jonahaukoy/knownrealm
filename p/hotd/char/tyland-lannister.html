Jason's twin and the smarter ledger: master of ships, then of coin — the man who quietly quartered the crown's gold so no one claimant could seize it all. Wars are won by such bookkeeping.

Where the page and the screen part

Tyland's financial maneuvering is covered in far greater specific detail in Fire & Blood than in the show's currently aired seasons, which have not yet reached the stage of the war where his stewardship of the treasury becomes narratively central.

Their journey

Where the two tellings send them to the same place the road runs whole; where they part, the screen keeps the left and the books the right.
