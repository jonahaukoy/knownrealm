Daeron Targaryen

House TargaryenThe Greens — the King's Party

Daeron the Daring, youngest of Alicent's sons, fostered at Oldtown as a Hightower squire and cupbearer — the best-natured of the brothers, with the she-dragon Tessarion, the Blue Queen.

A soul of House Targaryen, born at King's Landing, sworn to The Greens — the King's Party.

Season Two

The realm draws up for the battles to come: the Hightower host under Daeron, the Lannisters from the west, Cregan's northmen marching south, the Velaryon fleet massing for the Gullet.

How it ends for them

On the page. Fell in the fires of Second Tumbleton (F&B, ch. 7)

Daeron the Daring died at Second Tumbleton, the histories' kindest guess being smoke and fire in the burning camp — the best of Alicent's sons, and Tessarion the Blue Queen dying slow beside him.

The series has not yet reached this stage of the war; Daeron's fate remains ahead of the current seasons.

Fire & Blood places his death at Second Tumbleton, in the chaos and betrayal that follows Hugh Hammer and Ulf the White's defection from the greens' cause — the histories' kindest reconstruction has Daeron caught in the fires and confusion of the turncoats' camp, Tessarion burning slowly beside him, in what may be the single greatest loss of promise the greens suffer in the entire war.

Where the page and the screen part

Daeron receives comparatively little screen time in the show's aired seasons relative to his siblings, appearing mostly as a name and a distant presence in the Reach rather than a developed character — a gap Fire & Blood does not share, since the book follows his southern campaign in close and specific military detail across its second half.
