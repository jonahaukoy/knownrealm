Jaehaera Targaryen

House Targaryen

Jaehaerys's twin, a quiet, strange little girl — and, as the war spends her family, an ever more important piece on its board.

The fuller tale

Jaehaerys's twin sister is, for most of her short public life, exactly what the court needs her to be and nothing the sources bother recording beyond that: a quiet, watchful little girl whose importance rises sharply the moment her brother's murder leaves her the elder surviving child of the reigning king.

As the war grinds down her family's ranks of potential heirs, Jaehaera's marriageability becomes, with grim inevitability, a strategic asset debated by adults who rarely consult her — a small girl turned, by circumstance rather than any choice of her own, into an increasingly important piece on a board built entirely out of other people's ambitions.

The page and the screen

Jaehaera's role and even her eventual marriage are recorded with more certainty and consistency across the sources than most figures this young typically receive, though both the show's ongoing adaptation and Fire & Blood's own chroniclers treat her chiefly as a strategic object rather than a developed character in her own right — a gap neither telling entirely closes.
