The chapter retold

The section's title is the whole judgment: a reign measured in months and misery, purchased at a price that ruins the man who pays it. Rhaenyra, driven from King's Landing, flees to Dragonstone and into a trap, sold by her own garrison to Aegon II ÔÇö who has her brought to the yard where the dying Sunfyre still lives and orders the dragon to eat her before her son's eyes. Even Aegon's council calls it a blunder: a martyrdom instead of a punishment, the last image the Dance will leave the realm to argue over for three hundred years. It buys the king exactly half a year.

That half-year is only more dying. Second Tumbleton kills the Betrayer Hugh Hammer and the young Prince Daeron the Daring, the best of Alicent's sons, burned with his blue Tessarion; Addam of Hull dies there too, proving with his life that dragonseed is not treason's seed. And with Cregan Stark's northmen two days from the city and no dragons left worth the name, Aegon II is poisoned in his own litter by the very lords who crowned him ÔÇö 'let there be an end,' they agreed, the least mysterious murder in the whole history, and the plainest statement that the peace required the winner gone as surely as the loser.
