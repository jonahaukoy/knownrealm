The chapter retold

The war ends and the reckoning begins in the same breath. Cregan Stark arrives too late to fight and just in time to judge, and for six days the Wolf of the North holds King's Landing in all but name ÔÇö the Hour of the Wolf ÔÇö trying poisoners and turncloaks and taking the heads that the new king's councillors would rather have pardoned, including clever Larys Strong, last of his house, who refuses the Wall with a shrug. It is the closest thing to justice the Dance produces, and it lasts less than a week before being folded back into the ordinary business of a realm desperate to stop bleeding.

Peace, when it comes, is made of exhaustion rather than reconciliation. Aegon the Third, Rhaenyra's surviving son, is crowned and wed to Aegon II's daughter Jaehaera, the two poisoned halves of one family stitched together over a boy king who flinches from the very creatures his house conquered the world on. The dragons dwindle to a handful of sickly hatchlings and then, within his reign, to none ÔÇö the true legacy of the Dance, a dynasty that burned its own foundations so thoroughly that every Targaryen who follows, down to Aerys and Daenerys, inherits a throne with no dragons under it and only a name and the memory of fear to hold it up. What the whole thing was for, Fire & Blood admits, the maesters are still arguing.
