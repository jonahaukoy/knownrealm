The hour retold

An hour about who inherits a war when its king can no longer wear it. Aegon lives ÔÇö barely ÔÇö burned, broken, and drowned in milk of the poppy, and the green council quietly passes the rule not to his mother but to Aemond, handing the realm to the one hand in the family most certain to burn it. The realm learns a lesson it can't unlearn.
