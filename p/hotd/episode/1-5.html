The hour retold

A wedding hour, and everything about it is funereal. It opens with a murder disguised as an accident ÔÇö Daemon calling on the wife he never wanted at Runestone, and Lady Rhea Royce dead in the dirt with the stone that finished her in her husband's hand ÔÇö and it ends with a betrothal feast turned abattoir. In between, Viserys sails to Driftmark to mend the realm's two dragon houses, promising Rhaenyra to Laenor Velaryon and their someday-sons to the throne, and the two young people, each in love elsewhere, strike the only honest bargain in the room: a marriage of duty with the door left open.

The episode's title is the Hightower words, and the episode's last image explains them. Ser Criston Cole, refused as a paramour and unstrung by the vows he has already broken, beats Laenor's lover Ser Joffrey Lonmouth to death on the feast-hall floor while the court watches its future curdle. And Alicent arrives late, deliberately, in a gown of Hightower green ÔÇö the color Oldtown's beacon burns when it calls its banners to war. She says nothing. She doesn't need to. The two women who were once each other's only friend are now the two poles of a realm, and the whole hall reads the dress correctly.
