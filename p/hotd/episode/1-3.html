S1·E3 — Second of His Name

Viserys I Targaryen holds the throne Hand: Ser Otto Hightower

The hour retold

Three years on, the crack has become a fault line, and the hour stages it as a hunt. Aegon, Alicent's firstborn son, has his second nameday in the kingswood, and half the court arranges itself to hint that here — a boy, a Targaryen boy — is the heir the realm actually wants. Viserys drinks through it, cornered by his own decision, while Rhaenyra rides out furious, kills a boar in the dark with her own spear, and watches a white hart, the old omen of kingship, walk out of the wood uncrowned. The episode keeps offering signs and letting everyone read them wrong.

In the Stepstones the other war grinds toward its ugly resolution. The crown finally sends the aid it has withheld for years, and the shame of needing rescue drives Daemon to beat the messenger bloody with his own helm before doing the one thing no one can rescue him from. He rows toward the Crabfeeder's line under a peace banner, feigns surrender, and turns it into a slaughter — walking out of the caves dragging half of Craghas Drahar behind him, a three-year stalemate broken by a single unforgivable lie. It is Daemon distilled: the victory is total, the method is dishonorable, and the realm loves him more for it, not less.

What happens

  • Three years on: Aegon, Alicent's son, has his second nameday hunt in the kingswood — and half the court hints he should be heir.
  • Rhaenyra rides out angry, kills a boar in the dark with Ser Criston, and watches a white hart — the king's omen — walk away unkinged.
  • In the Stepstones the war goes badly: Craghas Crabfeeder bleeds the Velaryon host from his caves while the Triarchy holds the tides.
  • Viserys finally sends aid; the letter's arrival shames Daemon into fury — he beats the messenger with his helm.
  • Daemon rows to the Crabfeeder's line under a peace banner, feigns surrender, and butchers a path until Craghas is cut in half beneath Caraxes.
