S2·E6 — Smallfolk

Aegon II Targaryen, senseless — Aemond Targaryen, Prince Regent holds the throne Hand: Ser Criston Cole

The hour retold

The season's argument comes into focus: the war will be won not only with fire but with bread. Hunger bites King's Landing hard, and Rhaenyra and Mysaria answer it by feeding the city from black ships, so that the smallfolk begin, dangerously for the greens, to love the queen who feeds them over the king who hangs their fathers. It is the White Worm's finest work — turning a blockade's cruelty into a queen's mercy — and the first sign that Rhaenyra has weapons Aemond's council never thinks to count.

The hour's turn is on a Dragonstone slope, and it rewrites the war's rules. Ser Steffon Darklyn dies in fire trying to claim the riderless Seasmoke with pedigree and a knight's nerve — and then Seasmoke does its own choosing, hunting down the baseborn Addam of Hull and taking him alive into the sky. A dragon has chosen a bastard over a knight, and the implication is enormous: the blood of old Valyria ran wider than the great houses ever admitted, and it is riding fishing boats and working forges all over the realm. Aemond answers the blockade the only way he knows, burning black-sworn towns along the coast, while Daemon learns in the riverlands that terror recruits enemies faster than it recruits banners.

What happens

  • Hunger bites King's Landing; Rhaenyra and Mysaria feed the city from black ships, and the smallfolk begin to love the queen who feeds them.
  • Ser Steffon Darklyn dies in fire on Dragonstone's slopes, trying to claim the riderless Seasmoke for the blacks.
  • Seasmoke does his own choosing: he hunts down Addam of Hull on Driftmark and takes him into the sky alive.
  • In the riverlands, Daemon lets the Blackwoods loose on Bracken lands — and learns that terror recruits enemies faster than banners.
  • Aemond answers the blockade's noose the only way he knows: Vhagar burns black-sworn towns along the crownlands coast.
