S1·E9 — The Green Council

Aegon II Targaryen holds the throne Hand: Ser Otto Hightower

The hour retold

The king is dead and only the greens know it, and the penultimate hour is a coup conducted in a sealed castle before the body is cold. Otto Hightower's council — planning this for twenty years — moves to crown Aegon, and when old Lord Beesbury alone says the word 'treason,' Ser Criston Cole opens his throat on the council table, the Dance's first death paid before the war is even declared. The horror of the episode is administrative: a peace ending not with a battle but with a quorum, men who have known each other for decades agreeing to steal a crown between breakfast and supper.

The trouble is that the heir they mean to crown does not want it. Aegon is hunted through Flea Bottom like a stray, found hiding from his own coronation and dragged to the Dragonpit to be crowned before a mob with the Conqueror's crown and blade. And Rhaenys — locked up, courted for her dragon, escaping through the same crowds herded to watch — chooses the hour's defining act: bursting Meleys through the pit floor with the entire green court at her mercy, and then not burning them. It is the war's great road not taken, restraint offered by the one person with every reason to withhold it, and the show lets it hang there as the last mercy anyone will get.

What happens

  • The king is dead, and only the greens know. The Red Keep is sealed while Otto's council — planning this for years — moves to crown Aegon.
  • Old Lord Beesbury alone objects that this is treason; Ser Criston opens his throat on the council table.
  • Aegon is nowhere: a hunt through Flea Bottom finds the heir hiding from his own crown, dragged out by the White Worm's price.
  • Rhaenys is locked in her chambers, courted for Meleys — and escapes with Ser Erryk through the crowds being herded to the Dragonpit.
  • Aegon II is crowned before the mob with the Conqueror's crown and blade — until Meleys bursts through the pit floor, and Rhaenys, with the greens at her mercy, spares them and flies to Dragonstone.
