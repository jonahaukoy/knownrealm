The hour retold

The king is dead and only the greens know it, and the penultimate hour is a coup conducted in a sealed castle before the body is cold. The horror of the episode is administrative: a peace ending not with a battle but with a quorum, men who have known each other for decades agreeing to steal a crown between breakfast and supper.

The trouble is that the heir they mean to crown does not want it.
