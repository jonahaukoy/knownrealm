S1·E10 — The Black Queen

Aegon II Targaryen — and Rhaenyra, crowned in defiance holds the throne Hand: Ser Otto Hightower — for the greens; Daemon speaks for the blacks

The hour retold

The finale is about a queen refusing to be a rebel. Word of the usurpation reaches Dragonstone and the shock throws Rhaenyra into an early, killing labor; she buries a daughter, Visenya, the same day her crown is stolen, two thefts in one afternoon. A stolen circlet arrives with Ser Erryk, and Daemon sets her father's crown on her head on the shore — but the woman crowned spends the hour holding her own war back, answering Otto's peace terms with a monarch's discipline rather than a widow's fury, insisting on envoys where Daemon wants dragons.

The painted table counts the odds — the Vale, the North, the riverlands to win, thirteen dragons against the greens' four — and Rhaenyra keeps choosing statecraft anyway, sending her sons out as messengers and not warriors. Jacaerys flies north for the mountain and the snow; Lucerys flies to Storm's End, where Vhagar is already waiting. Above Shipbreaker Bay, Aemond's old game stops being a game: Vhagar takes Arrax and Lucerys both, whether her rider meant murder or only meant to frighten a boy. The season's last shot is Rhaenyra's face receiving the news, and the restraint she has held all hour going out of it. The peace her father died keeping dies with her son.

What happens

  • Word of the usurpation reaches Dragonstone; the shock sends Rhaenyra into early labor, and she buries a daughter, Visenya, the same day.
  • Ser Erryk Cargyll arrives with a stolen crown; Daemon sets Viserys's crown on Rhaenyra's head on the shore of Dragonstone.
  • The painted table counts its strength: the Vale, the North, the riverlands to win; thirteen dragons to the greens' four — and Daemon wants them flying now.
  • Otto comes under the peace banner with terms; Rhaenyra answers with the words of a queen, not a rebel — and holds Daemon's war back.
  • Jacaerys flies for the Eyrie and Winterfell, Lucerys for Storm's End — envoys, not warriors. At Storm's End waits Vhagar.
  • Above Shipbreaker Bay, Aemond's game stops being a game: Vhagar takes Arrax and Lucerys both — and Rhaenyra's face, hearing it, ends the peace.
