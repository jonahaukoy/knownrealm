House Hightower

Lords of Oldtown since before memory, keepers of the great lighthouse tower that gives them their name and their words. A white stone tower crowned with fire on smoke grey, they rule the oldest and richest city in Westeros, patrons of the Citadel and the Faith both ÔÇö and through Queen Alicent and Ser Otto, the green half of the realm's great quarrel.
